# Sparse reporting pays in recomputation cadence and label time · `plan:assayer:intent-ill-conditioning-is-paid-for-in-recomputation-cadence-and-per-label-cost`

Keeping (´claim:audit:ill-conditioning-is-paid-for-in-recomputation-cadence-and-per-label-cost´) requires an equal-width sparse and dense comparison which separates the cost of adaptive posterior maintenance from assessment construction, queue waiting, and unequal eligible work. No test yet mints or cites this claim.

## What the promise says, precisely · `sec:assayer:intent-ill-conditioning-is-paid-for-in-recomputation-cadence-and-per-label-cost-precision`

The comparison uses two converged worlds with the same configuration, registrations, report values, entity sequence, outcomes, eligibility, and accepted-label count. Each world registers nine Sentinels; six appear on every request, while the remaining three appear at three fixed positions in every hundred-request cycle in the sparse arm and on every request in the dense arm. Three of nine Sentinels reporting on three per cent of requests is the population fixed by (´claim:linalg:sparsely-reporting-sentinels-ill-condition-the-precision-matrix´).

Equal registration sets make the model widths equal even when request occupancy differs. Distinct deterministic report readings and rotating coordinates prevent the dense control from becoming collinear merely because every Sentinel is present.

After a rebuild, the sparse operational model must carry `PrecisionHealthDetail::kappa = Some(kappa)` with $\kappa(B) > 10^4$, while the dense operational model must carry a finite value below that boundary. The eigenvalue ratio, not `PrecisionHealthDetail::diagonal_ratio`, is the condition number, and it is absent until a rebuild has paid for the decomposition (´def:monitoring:condition-number´); sparse evidence is permitted to leave it large (´cav:limitation:conditioning´).

The cadence half is not yet an executable numeric assertion. The claim says that the “recomputation interval” becomes roughly three hundred labels, while the specification uses interval for `PrecisionHealthDetail::n_recompute_effective`: it starts at one thousand, halves after an adopted residual-driven rebuild subject to a floor of one hundred, and therefore visits one thousand, five hundred, two hundred and fifty, one hundred and twenty-five, and one hundred under the shipped policy (´tab:config:risk-model´) (´dec:posterior:adaptive-cadence´). Conditioning-growth and floored-count changes can rebuild before that counter interval, and a counter visit can measure without rebuilding (´alg:gaussian:condition-adaptive-recompute´) (´dec:posterior:recomputation-trigger´). The former `[250, 350]` median-gap band silently substituted realised rebuild spacing for the named interval and invented a tolerance for “roughly”; the witness must record both the effective-interval sequence and complete rebuild gaps, but must not choose either as the verdict until the claim's authority selects the quantity and tolerance.

The cost half is exact. An increase by a third to a half means that the sparse-to-dense median per-accepted-label duration lies in the inclusive band $[4/3, 3/2]$. Setup, report ingestion, assessment preparation, health reads, and fixture validation stay outside the measured region; the complete label publication and the joined dense model-update region are reported separately so the first keeps the public-path promise and the second attributes the difference to posterior maintenance.

The ordinary label path is quadratic in model width and the resource table contains no Cholesky-recomputation row or cadence amortisation (´tab:resource:label-cost´). A recomputation additionally takes a spectrum, applies the spectral floor, factors the precision matrix, measures the offered inverse, and may adopt it, so the ratio must be measured rather than inferred from asymptotic classes (´alg:runtime:update-path´) (´dec:posterior:recomputation-trigger´).

Correctness accompanies cost: every prepared label is accepted, every post-window assessment is finite, each adopted rebuild's synchronisation reading is strictly below `Tolerances::precision_sync_ceiling` at that model's width, no precision alarm or cascade terminus appears, and the label path remains live (´def:monitoring:synchronisation-error´) (´tab:assayer:harness-scenario-tolerances´). A run that is fast because it stopped learning does not keep the promise.

The standing testing plan contains no gap entry covering this promise.

## What the code offers today · `sec:assayer:intent-ill-conditioning-is-paid-for-in-recomputation-cadence-and-per-label-cost-code-today`

The finished harness exposes one `World` through `scenario_with_config`, with deterministic seeds, custom `AssayerConfig`, named Sentinel registration and report ingestion, request construction, assessment, label submission, and the queue-specific `World::flush_observations` and `World::flush_labels` barriers (´tab:assayer:harness-implementation-library-roster´). No sleep, poll, or test-authored deadline is needed.

Long streams can now implement `PlaybackRow` and run through `playback` with a `PlaybackBarrierPolicy`, per-row `PlaybackCheckpoint` callbacks, and `PlaybackProgress`; the runner owns barrier cadence and liveness diagnostics (´dec:harness:declarative-playback´). The sparse and dense streams need observation settlement before label publication during training and label publication alone for already prepared measurement rows.

`World::activate_label_update_recorder` and `World::drain_label_update_duration` already measure the joined dense model-update region and make the drain cross `World::flush_labels`. Their focused witness proves that the duration comes only from completed real-engine publications (´test:crate:label-update-recorder-returns-only-a-completed-dense-publication-duration´), and the existing `performance` benchmark consumes the same recorder for its dense-update identifier (´test:bench:bench-label-publication´). Timing instrumentation is therefore landed rather than missing.

After `World::flush_labels`, the public `Assayer::full_health_report` reached through `World::assayer` returns owned per-model `PrecisionHealthDetail` rows containing `kappa`, `diagonal_ratio`, `last_measurement_labels`, `n_recompute_effective`, `measurements`, `cholesky_recomputes`, synchronisation readings, alarms, and cascade-terminus counts (´dec:health:tiered-queries´). The generic test-support probes deliberately omit precision state, so widening `PublishedModelBlock` is neither needed nor permitted (´dec:harness:probe-contract´) (´cav:retention:probe-boundary´).

`TrainedStateFixture` is landed, but its fixed score-verified population guards class counts and held-out separation rather than this witness's equal-width Sentinel occupancy and conditioning (´tab:assayer:harness-implementation-library-roster´). The sparse/dense pair therefore still needs a subject-specific guarded fixture whose measured baseline is separate from the cadence and cost verdicts (´dec:harness:guarded-fixtures´) (´dec:harness:separate-validation´).

The integration test `the_configured_cadence_does_not_move_the_synchronisation_error` exercises configured intervals, adaptive shortening, rebuild counters, and elapsed execution, but it neither constructs the equal-width sparse/dense pair nor asserts this claim (´test:integration:the-configured-cadence-does-not-move-the-synchronisation-error´). The integration test `a_dense_competitive_schedule_leaves_every_precision_matrix_factorisable` supplies the nearest long-running correctness pattern and rejects a factorisation terminus without establishing either promised ratio (´test:integration:a-dense-competitive-schedule-leaves-every-precision-matrix-factorisable´). The claim remains unkept.

## The witness · `sec:assayer:intent-ill-conditioning-is-paid-for-in-recomputation-cadence-and-per-label-cost-witness`

The correctness and cadence witness belongs to integration target `synchronisation_drift` as `synchronisation_drift::ill_conditioning_is_paid_for_in_recomputation_cadence_and_per_label_cost`. The cost comparison belongs to the existing Criterion target `performance` as `performance::bench_ill_conditioning_cost`, because maintained wall-time evidence uses the shared performance route rather than an ignored stopwatch assertion (´dec:harness:performance-benchmarks´). Both consume the same guarded pair and typed rows; the claim is kept only when the selected cadence reading passes its authorised acceptance rule and the performance report places the paired ratio inside $[4/3, 3/2]$.

- Setup: construct dense and sparse `World` values through `scenario_with_config` from one `AssayerConfig` and seed, register the same nine named Sentinels, ingest distinct deterministic reports, and cross `World::flush_observations` and `World::flush_labels` only for the queues each setup step feeds.
- Training stimulus: express the balanced deterministic request-and-label stream as `PlaybackRow` values and execute it with `playback`; use a `PlaybackBarrierPolicy` ordered as observation settlement followed by label publication, and use `PlaybackProgress` only as liveness evidence (´dec:harness:declarative-playback´).
- Occupancy stimulus: attach all nine Sentinel coordinates in the dense arm; attach six in every sparse-arm row and the other three at exactly three fixed positions in each hundred-row cycle, using the same coordinates whenever a sparse Sentinel is present.
- Fixture gate: return the pair only after `World::observed_runtime_layout` proves equal width and the owned full-health readings prove equal accepted-label counts, converged state, at least one rebuild, sparse true condition number above $10^4$, dense true condition number below $10^4$, and no stopped path, alarm, or degradation that invalidates the comparison (´dec:harness:guarded-fixtures´).
- Cadence observation: use a `PlaybackCheckpoint` after each settled measurement row to read the operational `PrecisionHealthDetail` once, record every `cholesky_recomputes` change at its accepted-label index, retain only complete event-to-event gaps, and record the contemporaneous `n_recompute_effective` sequence. Do not turn either series into a pass criterion until the cadence ambiguity is resolved.
- Cost preparation: outside every measured sample, prepare matched pending assessments and their labels through playback, check equal eligibility and model width, and create fresh guarded worlds so one sample's adaptive state does not leak into the next.
- Cost observation: alternate arm order, time complete publication through the label barrier under Criterion, and bracket the same rows with `World::activate_label_update_recorder` and `World::drain_label_update_duration` to attribute the result to the joined model-update region. Divide each duration by its accepted-label count, retain paired sparse-to-dense ratios, and report their median with Criterion's distribution rather than treating playback progress as latency evidence (´dec:harness:performance-benchmarks´).
- Correctness assertion: require every label accounted for, finite post-window assessments, each adopted rebuild below `Tolerances::precision_sync_ceiling`, no alarms or cascade termini, and a live model owner. The fixture guard establishes setup; these assertions remain the result oracle (´dec:harness:separate-validation´).
- Diagnostic output: report each arm's true condition number, diagonal bound, effective-interval sequence, measurement and rebuild deltas, complete rebuild gaps, complete-publication duration, recorded model-update duration, duration per accepted label, paired ratio, and health verdict.

The fails-before for the cost half is an implementation that ignores conditioning and visits only at the configured counter cadence: its sparse model-update duration approaches the dense control and the lower ratio bound rejects it. An implementation that rebuilds on every label exceeds the upper ratio bound; one that drops labels or stops the owner fails accounting and liveness. The cadence fails-before remains intentionally unstated until the promise identifies whether it governs `n_recompute_effective` or realised rebuild spacing and supplies the acceptance tolerance.

## Risks and open questions · `sec:assayer:intent-ill-conditioning-is-paid-for-in-recomputation-cadence-and-per-label-cost-risks`

**Observation (Realised cadence is not the effective interval field)** · `obs:assayer:intent-ill-conditioning-is-paid-for-in-recomputation-cadence-and-per-label-cost-cadence-semantics`

`PrecisionHealthDetail::n_recompute_effective` takes the policy's discrete values, while conditioning growth and floored-count movement can rebuild before it and a counter visit can rebuild nothing. The intent's phrase “roughly every three hundred labels” could name either the field near that figure or realised complete rebuild gaps; the existing specification does not choose. The implementing work must not infer the choice from whichever reading happens to pass.

**Observation (Wall time needs a controlled execution home)** · `obs:assayer:intent-ill-conditioning-is-paid-for-in-recomputation-cadence-and-per-label-cost-timing-home`

The label-update recorder isolates the model-update region, but it still records elapsed processor time and supplies no distribution by itself. Criterion warm-up, paired fresh fixtures, alternating arm order, confidence intervals, a quiet reference runner, and one rerun of a suspicious result are therefore part of the evidence; no default test or playback liveness deadline becomes a latency oracle (´dec:harness:performance-benchmarks´).

**Observation (The numeric bands have one authority)** · `obs:assayer:intent-ill-conditioning-is-paid-for-in-recomputation-cadence-and-per-label-cost-band-authority`

The cost band $[4/3, 3/2]$ follows exactly from “a third to a half.” No specification sentence supplies a tolerance around three hundred, so the former `[250, 350]` range has been removed rather than fitted to current behaviour. The claim's authority must state the cadence quantity and tolerance before that half can accept or reject an implementation.

**Observation (Condition readings are rebuild-aged)** · `obs:assayer:intent-ill-conditioning-is-paid-for-in-recomputation-cadence-and-per-label-cost-reading-age`

`PrecisionHealthDetail::kappa` is `None` before the first rebuild and thereafter describes the precision matrix at the last rebuild, while `PrecisionHealthDetail::diagonal_ratio` is current and only a lower bound (´def:monitoring:condition-number´). The fixture records both at each rebuild checkpoint and begins the observation window immediately after one, so a stale true reading or fresh proxy cannot silently stand for current conditioning.

**Observation (Equal width does not mean equal assessment work)** · `obs:assayer:intent-ill-conditioning-is-paid-for-in-recomputation-cadence-and-per-label-cost-cost-isolation`

The sparse arm carries fewer occupied Sentinel slots even though both models have the same width. Assessment preparation therefore stays outside the measured label-publication sample, equal eligibility keeps the updated model set fixed, and the recorder identifies the posterior-update share. The complete-publication ratio remains the promise's verdict; the recorder is attribution evidence rather than a substitute subject.

## Acceptance · `sec:assayer:intent-ill-conditioning-is-paid-for-in-recomputation-cadence-and-per-label-cost-acceptance`

Implementation is ready for review when integration target `synchronisation_drift` contains `synchronisation_drift::ill_conditioning_is_paid_for_in_recomputation_cadence_and_per_label_cost`, Criterion target `performance` contains `performance::bench_ill_conditioning_cost`, both consume the guarded `IllConditioningPair`, and their documentation attaches the intent claim to the combined cadence, cost, and correctness evidence.

The integration report carries registered-Sentinel count, accepted-label count, equal width, convergence reading, operational-model true condition number and diagonal bound, effective-interval sequence, measurement and rebuild deltas, complete rebuild gaps, synchronisation ceiling and reading, alarm and terminus counts, and liveness verdict for both arms. Acceptance also requires every label applied, finite post-window assessments, synchronisation below the width-scaled threshold after adopted rebuilds, zero alarms and cascade termini, and a live model owner; fixed-cadence, every-label-rebuild, and label-dropping failures are each identified without adding a lint exemption or an executed mutation.
