# Keeping an Unreported Outcome Axis at Its Prior · `plan:assayer:intent-an-axis-nobody-reports-stays-at-its-prior`

This plan keeps the promise that an outcome axis nobody reports stays at its prior (´claim:lifespan:an-axis-nobody-reports-stays-at-its-prior´); the witness observes the configured prior through assessment-only traffic and clean removal on retirement.

## What the promise says, precisely · `sec:assayer:intent-an-axis-nobody-reports-stays-at-its-prior-promise`

For a newly registered axis $a$, the model starts at the full post-registration width with zero mean $\mu_a$, precision $B_a = \lambda_\text{prior} I$, and covariance $\Sigma_a = \lambda_\text{prior}^{-1} I$. The per-axis model has the same Gaussian posterior and lifecycle algebra as the risk models (´def:axis:per-axis-model´), and extension introduces an independent prior block with zero cross-terms (´thm:gaussian:extension´).

The candidate axis contributes $r_a = 2n \cdot \mathbb{1}[\text{spatial}] + 3D$ coordinates, plus declared interactions, for $n$ registered Sentinels and $D$ registered identity dimensions (´alg:registry:axis-registration´). Registration adds those coordinates to the operational, sister, and every pre-existing outcome-axis model with mean zero, precision $\lambda_\text{prior} I$, covariance $\lambda_\text{prior}^{-1} I$, and zero coupling to the surviving coordinates; the anchor owns no candidate coordinates and remains unchanged (´tab:registry:axis-operations´).

Assessment alone may move observational geometry but no outcome-learned state (´dec:ordering:evidence-authority´). Consequently, after any finite assessment-only sequence, the candidate model and every candidate-owned block in the other full-dimensional models retain their registration values; the witness submits no labels, so it does not conflate this claim with partial outcome reporting (´req:registry:partial-outcome-reporting´).

Retirement destroys the candidate model, drops its outcome memory and standardisation entries, and removes every coordinate it contributed (´alg:registry:axis-deregistration´) (´dec:construction:destructive-deregistration´). Because the candidate block remains independent, the Schur correction is zero: each surviving mean and precision returns exactly to its pre-registration value under the marginalisation identity (´thm:gaussian:marginalisation´), while the refactored covariance is compared under the declared one-shot numerical budget (´dec:posterior:always-refactor´).

The witness separates prior precision from compression scale by configuring $\lambda_\text{prior}=1/4$ while retaining the registration helper's $\kappa_{a,0}=1$. Prior precision is a host-settable model quantity (´tab:config:risk-model´), whereas the axis registration schema defines $\kappa_{a,0}$ only as the initial compression scale (´schema:registry:axis-record´).

## What the code offers today · `sec:assayer:intent-an-axis-nobody-reports-stays-at-its-prior-code-today`

`scenario_with_config` constructs the real engine with an explicit `AssayerConfig`; `World::register_sentinel`, `World::register_axis`, `World::derive_default`, and `World::deregister_axis` drive the complete public lifecycle and assessment path. `World::settle_cold_ramp_with` establishes the observation precondition, and the scenario keeps time fixed by making no call to `World::advance` or `World::travel_to`. These are present members of the finished harness roster rather than pending skeleton work (´tab:assayer:harness-implementation-library-roster´).

The long assessment stimulus can implement `PlaybackRow` and run through `playback` with a `PlaybackBarrierPolicy` containing `PlaybackBarrier::FlushObservations`; `PlaybackProgress` then identifies an incomplete row without turning elapsed time into an assertion (´dec:harness:declarative-playback´). The final model readings come from `World::published_model_block`, which crosses the model-owner publication barrier, loads once, and returns an owned `PublishedModelBlock` under the probe contract (´dec:harness:probe-contract´).

`World::observed_runtime_layout` supplies the published block widths, while `dimension_width` independently evaluates the specification's dimension formula and `regularised_schur_complement` independently evaluates the retirement formula. The oracle tier records both computations as specification routes (´dec:harness:oracle-tier´). Published precision remains deliberately absent, so the witness evaluates the expected precision against the published covariance instead of widening the probe surface (´dec:retention:precision-excluded´).

The production registration handler extends existing models with `ModelConfig::lambda_prior` but constructs the new axis model with `OutcomeAxisRegistration::initial_kappa` in the prior-precision argument and also stores that value as the compression scale. A configured prior unequal to the compression scale therefore makes the candidate model's wrong starting covariance observable immediately.

No test cites the intent claim. The seeded axis-lifecycle pack already proves exact extension of existing full-dimensional models and regularised-Schur retirement after training the target axis (´test:crate:axis-lifecycle-seeded-sweep´), and the assessment write-set pack proves generically that an assessment leaves published outcome-taught state unchanged (´test:crate:assessment-write-set-seeded-sweep´). The integration round trip checks name removal (´test:integration:register-deregister-axis-round-trip´), the churn cycle checks repeated finite assessment across both axis shapes (´test:integration:axis-register-deregister-churn-cycle´), and the ledger witness checks that registration does not perturb the risk basis (´test:integration:outcome-axis-registration-does-not-perturb-risk-basis´); none combines a non-default configured prior, an unreported candidate, assessment-only playback, and a survivor-state comparison, so the promise remains unkept.

## The witness · `sec:assayer:intent-an-axis-nobody-reports-stays-at-its-prior-witness`

**Decision (One barriered lifecycle witnesses the whole promise)** · `dec:assayer:intent-an-axis-nobody-reports-stays-at-its-prior-one-barriered-lifecycle`

The witness belongs in the `lifecycle_algebra` integration target beside the existing axis lifecycle round trip (´test:integration:register-deregister-axis-round-trip´). It uses the public lifecycle and assessment surface, the finished owned probes, and test-local matrix comparisons; it adds no production snapshot field and no shared harness export.

- **Setup.** Build a fixed-seed `scenario_with_config` with $\lambda_\text{prior}=1/4$, no identity dimensions or interaction templates, one registered but silent Sentinel, and one incumbent non-spatial axis. Settle the cold ramp through `World::settle_cold_ramp_with`, then capture the survivor baseline and register a spatial candidate through `World::register_axis`; `dimension_width` confirms that the candidate adds exactly the two Sentinel-owned coordinates required by the dimension formula (´tab:feature:dimension-formula´).
- **Registration observation.** Read the operational, sister, anchor, incumbent-axis, and candidate-axis publications through `World::published_model_block`, and read the layout once through `World::observed_runtime_layout`. The candidate model must have zero mean and covariance $4I$ over its entire full-width state, and the Frobenius departure of $(1/4)I\Sigma_a$ from the identity must stay below `DEFAULT_TOLERANCES.precision_sync_ceiling(width)`. The operational, sister, and incumbent models must retain every old cell bit-identically and contain a two-coordinate zero-mean, $4I$ covariance block with zero cross-couplings, while the anchor's numerical payload remains bit-identical.
- **Stimulus.** Materialise $n_\text{init}+1$ assessment-only rows, each of which calls `World::derive_default` for a stable entity and asserts that the candidate's raw prediction remains zero. Drive the rows through `playback` with `PlaybackBarrierPolicy` set to `PlaybackBarrier::FlushObservations`, no labels, and no time movement; the count is derived from the configured cold-ramp horizon and places traffic beyond one complete ramp rather than choosing an unexplained literal (´dec:harness:declarative-playback´).
- **Traffic observation.** After playback, take the same owned model and layout readings. Ignore publication version and layout-generation metadata, then require every candidate mean and covariance cell, every candidate-owned foreign block, the anchor's numerical payload, model membership, and layout width to agree with the registration checkpoint; recompute the same expected-precision product and apply the same width-scaled ceiling. The generic assessment write-set sweep is supporting evidence for the no-write boundary, while this checkpoint makes the candidate-axis case non-vacuous (´test:crate:assessment-write-set-seeded-sweep´).
- **Retirement assertion.** Capture the full-dimensional models immediately before retirement, derive the two removed positions from the observed registration transition, and pass each analytically expected prior precision plus the configured Schur offset to `regularised_schur_complement`. Invert the oracle precision through a test-local factorisation, deregister through `World::deregister_axis`, then require the candidate name and model to be absent, the layout to equal the pre-registration width, retained means to agree exactly, each survivor covariance to agree with that independently derived covariance and its pre-registration baseline under the named budgets, and the anchor's numerical payload to equal its baseline; the independent block makes the oracle's correction zero (´thm:gaussian:marginalisation´) (´dec:harness:oracle-tier´).
- **Fails-before.** The first candidate checkpoint fails in the unmodified implementation: `OutcomeAxisRegistration::initial_kappa=1` constructs $B=I$ and $\Sigma=I$ even though `ModelConfig::lambda_prior=1/4` requires $B=(1/4)I$ and $\Sigma=4I$. A broken assessment path that mutates an unreported model fails the post-playback checkpoint, and retained axis state or a wrong removal set fails the final model, layout, or survivor comparison.

Exact zeros, membership, dimensions, untouched payload cells, and the registration extension use `DEFAULT_TOLERANCES.exact`. Expected-precision products use `DEFAULT_TOLERANCES.precision_sync_ceiling(width)`, and post-retirement covariance comparison uses `DEFAULT_TOLERANCES.default` because it is a one-shot matrix comparison rather than an accumulated recurrence; every budget comes from the harness tolerance register rather than a test-local fit (´tab:assayer:harness-scenario-tolerances´).

The witness does not assert that ordinary prior-backed prediction uncertainty is two. The prior-only fallback formula gives two at fixed time for $\kappa_{a,0}=1$ and $\lambda_\text{prior}=1/4$, but that formula applies when axis evaluation cannot be made; a successful prediction instead depends on $\phi^\top\Sigma_a\phi$ (´def:axis:prior-only-prediction´) (´def:axis:per-axis-model´).

No shared harness item is missing.

## Risks and open questions · `sec:assayer:intent-an-axis-nobody-reports-stays-at-its-prior-risks-open-questions`

**Observation (The axis prior has two plausible configuration homes)** · `obs:assayer:intent-an-axis-nobody-reports-stays-at-its-prior-prior-source`

The intent claim and registration algorithm use $\lambda_\text{prior}$, and the registration schema offers no axis-specific precision field (´claim:lifespan:an-axis-nobody-reports-stays-at-its-prior´) (´alg:registry:axis-registration´) (´schema:registry:axis-record´). The reference-configuration prose nevertheless says that an outcome axis's Schur offset uses the precision its own registration set rather than the tabulated model prior (´tab:config:risk-model´). The witness follows the claim and the schema by using the global configured prior; acceptance must record whether that conflicting sentence is corrected or an explicit axis-prior field is introduced before the production fix is treated as settled.

**Observation (Assessment traffic and omitted labels are distinct stimuli)** · `obs:assayer:intent-an-axis-nobody-reports-stays-at-its-prior-assessments-versus-labels`

The intent claim quantifies assessments, so the witness submits no labels. Labels that report another axis while omitting the candidate exercise the separate partial-reporting requirement and also update the other models; folding them into this test would make a failure ambiguous between assessment authority, omission semantics, forgetting, and the prior-lifetime claim (´req:registry:partial-outcome-reporting´).

**Observation (The exactness boundary follows the algebra)** · `obs:assayer:intent-an-axis-nobody-reports-stays-at-its-prior-exactness-boundary`

The published probe does not expose working precision, so the integration witness checks the expected precision against the published covariance through the specification's width-scaled synchronisation ceiling. A future promise specifically about the private stored matrix would require a direct crate-level witness rather than a wider probe (´dec:harness:probe-contract´) (´dec:retention:precision-excluded´); this witness still catches the present defect because construction initialises precision and covariance from the same wrong argument.

**Observation (Determinism excludes unrelated maintenance)** · `obs:assayer:intent-an-axis-nobody-reports-stays-at-its-prior-determinism-boundary`

The scenario registers no identity dimension, advances no scenario time, and submits no label. Playback crosses `PlaybackBarrier::FlushObservations` because assessments enqueue observation work, while each model probe crosses the label-and-command publication barrier it owns; no identity-maintenance queue exists in the fixture. The silent Sentinel makes the candidate's spatial block non-empty, and the incumbent axis makes extension into another axis model non-vacuous (´dec:harness:no-ad-hoc-waits´).

## Acceptance · `sec:assayer:intent-an-axis-nobody-reports-stays-at-its-prior-acceptance`

The implementing change corrects the candidate constructor and adds `crate::an_axis_nobody_reports_stays_at_its_prior` to the `lifecycle_algebra` integration target. That test cites the intent claim, uses the existing harness and oracle surfaces without adding an excluded precision probe, and leaves the existing seeded lifecycle and assessment-authority packs green (´test:crate:axis-lifecycle-seeded-sweep´) (´test:crate:assessment-write-set-seeded-sweep´).

Its report names the configured $\lambda_\text{prior}$ and $\kappa_{a,0}$ sentinels, the `dimension_width` result and two-coordinate delta, the derived assessment horizon, the `PlaybackBarrier::FlushObservations` policy, the publication barriers crossed by the probes, and the exact, width-scaled, and one-shot assertion groups.

Its evidence includes the fails-before candidate covariance, passing registration and post-playback prior checkpoints after repair, the passing regularised-Schur survivor comparison after retirement, absence of the candidate name and model, restoration of the baseline layout width, and unchanged anchor state.

Acceptance also records the disposition of the specification's axis-prior sentence. If registration gains an explicit prior-precision field, the test sets it independently of compression scale; otherwise the constructor and the sentence both follow the global `ModelConfig::lambda_prior` that the intent claim names.
