# Patient label corruption stays bounded and silent · `plan:assayer:intent-one-corrupted-label-in-two-hundred-costs-almost-nothing`

Keeping (´claim:wellness:one-corrupted-label-in-two-hundred-costs-almost-nothing´) establishes through the public surface that sparse, distributed label corruption raises no integrity alarm, reduces rank discrimination only slightly, and reaches a stationary cost instead of accumulating across a long stream.

## What the promise says, precisely · `sec:assayer:intent-one-corrupted-label-in-two-hundred-costs-almost-nothing-precision`

The measured horizon contains exactly ten thousand live assess–label cycles after a clean burn-in. Every contiguous block of two hundred cycles contains one deliberately inverted reported valence, giving fifty corrupted labels, while a clean control receives the same requests in the same order with every true valence intact.

Each block contains forty truly adverse and one hundred sixty truly benign outcomes. Corruption changes one benign outcome to adverse, so the rate is exactly one in two hundred and the class mix makes the discrimination cost large enough to read against the promised band rather than lost in the movement of a learning model's own scores.

The stream rotates two hundred entity keys through every block. Each entity receives fifty measured labels, and the fifty corruptions land on distinct entities, so corruption can remove at most one agreement from any entity's measured history, leaving a touched entity at `49 / 50 = 0.98` where the clean control reads one. The specification flags an entity whose concordance falls below the aggregate rank discrimination by more than the configured `0.25`, once the five-label gate is reached (´alg:monitoring:per-entity-concordance´) (´tab:config:monitoring´). Aggregate discrimination cannot exceed one, so the deficit is at most `0.02` however the aggregate moves, and the flag is far from firing.

The diagnostic compares the sign of the reported outcome with the sign of the risk estimate (´alg:monitoring:per-entity-concordance´), so a totally ordered surface reads one only when the two classes also fall on opposite sides of the estimate's zero. Ordering alone does not give that, and a stationary fixture that put both classes on one side would read a constant concordance near the adverse rate and would keep the promise vacuously. The clean control's reading of one is what establishes the sign condition, and it is measured rather than assumed.

The model drift accumulators subtract the configured allowance `kappa_drift = 0.1` on every step and reset only after a strict crossing of `h = 10` (´alg:monitoring:drift-cusums´). The Sentinel alarm–outcome accumulators subtract `kappa_lab = 0.02` and take a disagreement as an indicator, so one corrupted label — a reported adverse outcome on a quiet coordinate — leaves the quiet-alarm-adverse accumulator at `1 - kappa_lab = 0.98`, and forty-nine matching observations return it to zero (´def:monitoring:alarm-outcome-cusums´) (´tab:config:monitoring´). Placing the corrupted pulse first in each block leaves one hundred ninety-nine such observations before the block boundary is read.

Rank discrimination is the tie-corrected AUC over assessment-time predictions and eventual outcomes (´alg:monitoring:auc´). The default calibration buffer retains two thousand rows and the periodic refit runs every two hundred labels, so each two-thousand-label checkpoint in this stationary fixture contains four hundred true adverse rows, ten corrupted benign rows reported as adverse, and one thousand five hundred ninety correctly reported benign rows (´constr:platt:buffer´) (´tab:platt:refit-cadence´).

Clean AUC is `1.0` exactly, because total separation of the keyed surface puts every truly adverse row above every truly benign one. The corrupted figure is not fixed by the class tallies alone. Its four hundred true adverse rows still win every comparison, but its ten corrupted rows are drawn from the benign population and score inside it, so the corrupted reading is `(400 + 10q) / 410`, where `q` is the mean fraction of the one thousand five hundred ninety reported-benign rows that a corrupted row outscores, a tie counting one half as the specification's tie correction requires (´alg:monitoring:auc´). The loss is therefore `(1 - q) * 10 / 410`: it is `5 / 410`, about `0.0122`, when the corrupted rows sit at the middle of that population, and it reaches the promised band's edges at `q = 0.59` and `q = 0.18`. The class tallies fix everything in that expression except `q`, which the block layout fixes, so the witness measures `q` rather than assuming the middle.

The operational model forgets with a label-indexed rate of `0.9995`, while the sister and anchor use `0.9998`; their approximate label half-lives are fourteen hundred and thirty-five hundred respectively (´tab:risk:forgetting-rates´). Time and label decay combine into one factor per model per label (´dec:posterior:combined-factor´). The witness never moves scenario time, and time moves only through the scenario verbs (´entry:assayer:harness-scenario-time´), so the time-indexed factor stays at one and the whole of the forgetting under test is label-indexed.

The leverage cap limits a single observation's precision increase along its own direction to a factor of six at the default safety factor of five (´prop:update:leverage-bound´). The implementation reduces the effective weight before mutation rather than repairing a full-weight update afterwards (´dec:posterior:leverage-before´).

The measurable promise is the conjunction: every two-thousand-label checkpoint keeps the AUC loss between `0.01` and `0.02`, no per-entity concordance flag rises, no discrimination-instability or feature-stable-drift flag rises, no corruption-only drift reset appears, and every alarm–outcome accumulator is back at zero at the block boundary. This is the patient-corruption blind spot stated by the specification rather than a claim that the labels are detected and corrected (´cav:limitation:patient-corruption´) (´cav:monitoring:integrity-scope´).

## What the code offers today · `sec:assayer:intent-one-corrupted-label-in-two-hundred-costs-almost-nothing-code-today`

Waiting is a named barrier everywhere outside `testing::liveness`, so no step of this witness may sleep, poll, or set its own deadline (´dec:harness:no-ad-hoc-waits´).

`testing::playback` interprets subject-owned implementations of `testing::PlaybackRow` under an ordered `testing::PlaybackBarrierPolicy`, invokes each declared `testing::PlaybackCheckpoint` exactly once at its row boundary and after that row's barriers, advances `testing::PlaybackProgress` once per completed row, and treats a bounded batch as a cost boundary rather than a shared view (´dec:harness:declarative-playback´) (´entry:assayer:harness-tape-runner´) (´cor:ordering:tape-batches´). ## The witness · `sec:assayer:intent-one-corrupted-label-in-two-hundred-costs-almost-nothing-witness`

The `calibration_discrimination_convergence` integration target is the destination for this corruption-tolerance witness and carries its generated test index, because rank discrimination is the promise's terminal result (´dec:harness:convergence-split´). It constructs clean and corrupted worlds from the same explicit configuration and seed, registers one Sentinel on each, ingests identical two-region reports, and settles both cold standardisation ramps with `World::settle_cold_ramp_with` on the same quiet and loud request shapes.

The setup drives an identical two-thousand-label clean burn-in through both worlds, using distinct one-use entity keys so no burn-in key reaches the concordance gate. It then measures its own precondition and reports the measured baseline beside the declared one before the measured horizon opens: both ramps in service, both aggregate readings present, matched publication and label counts, and a keyed surface whose two true classes are totally ordered (´dec:harness:guarded-fixtures´). It captures drift-reset counters and full-health readings at the same point, so construction and calibration transients cannot be charged to the corruption phase, and it keeps that guard separate from the result oracle below (´dec:harness:separate-validation´).

The tape divides each measured run into fifty blocks of two hundred rows. Position zero is benign and is the only inverted label in the corrupted world; the adverse positions are fixed at declared offsets within the block; the remaining positions are benign; entity assignment rotates by block so each block visits every entity once and each corruption lands on a new entity. No identity dimension is registered, so the entity key reaches the concordance tracker and not the feature vector, and every benign request is feature-identical. The spread inside the reported-benign population is therefore the model's own movement across the buffer, and where a row sits in that spread is decided by its offset in the block.

Each row is a subject-owned `testing::PlaybackRow` (´dec:harness:declarative-playback´). It builds matched requests with the same entity and Sentinel coordinate, derives once on each world and records the assessment-time score it read, reports the true valence to the clean world and the scheduled valence to the corrupted world, and returns without settling anything. The runner then crosses `World::flush_labels` on both worlds and advances progress, so neither world derives its next request against an unpublished label. The coordinate is quiet for a true benign outcome and loud for a true adverse outcome, independent of whether the reported valence is corrupted.

A `testing::PlaybackCheckpoint` is declared at each block's last row. It reads the full report and the compact summary on both worlds and records the clean and corrupted per-entity label counts, fractions and flags, the tracker's lifetime eviction count, the directional alarm–outcome arrays, the per-model drift states, the drift-reset counts since the captured baseline, the feature-stable-drift flag from the compact tier, and the discrimination-instability flag from the full one.

Every tenth block's checkpoint falls on a two-thousand-label boundary, which is also a refit boundary, and additionally requires both aggregate readings to be present. It folds its own recorded prediction-outcome pairs for the buffer's span through `testing::pairwise_rank`, at the configured minimum class count and at the tie tolerance the published route reads, and compares that value with the published figure: two readings of one sample over the same rows, which is exactly what the declared AUC tolerance bounds (´tab:assayer:harness-scenario-tolerances´). It then compares the clean reading with total separation, the clean-minus-corrupted loss with the inclusive `[0.01, 0.02]` band, and records the corrupted rows' mean win fraction against the reported-benign rows so a loss near an edge names its cause.

The assertion joins all observations. Exactly fifty labels are inverted; every measured entity carries fifty labels, reading one in the clean world and one in the corrupted world except for the fifty touched entities, which read `49 / 50`; no entity is flagged and the tracker's eviction count is zero, so no flag was lost with an evicted entity; alarm–outcome accumulators are zero at each block boundary; the corrupted world emits no drift reset beyond the clean control and raises neither integrity flag; and all five two-thousand-label AUC checkpoints remain in the same loss band rather than widening with elapsed blocks.

Liveness stays evidence rather than assertion. `PlaybackProgress` names the first incomplete row, the shared progress watcher and the model-owner panic hook turn a stall into an attributable failure, and no elapsed time enters an acceptance condition (´cav:harness:progress-not-latency´).

The fails-before is concrete. An implementation that stops subtracting the Page allowance retains and compounds one pulse per block; one that flags any per-entity disagreement marks the fifty touched entities; one that omits the drift allowance emits corruption-only resets; one that lets the recent window or aggregate buffer drift from configuration changes the checkpoint arithmetic; one that drops the tie correction moves the published figure away from the oracle's fold over the same rows; and one that replays or applies corrupted updates without the declared forgetting and leverage protections drives a later AUC checkpoint beyond the two-hundredths ceiling.

## Risks and open questions · `sec:assayer:intent-one-corrupted-label-in-two-hundred-costs-almost-nothing-risks`

**Observation (The run horizon is not the AUC sample size)** · `obs:assayer:intent-one-corrupted-label-in-two-hundred-costs-almost-nothing-window-horizon`

Ten thousand labels exercise repeated exposure and model memory, while the published aggregate AUC reads only the latest two thousand calibration rows. The stationary block composition makes every full buffer an exact miniature of the measured horizon; describing the final AUC as computed over ten thousand rows would contradict the implemented bounded buffer.

**Observation (The asymmetric flip makes the promised loss measurable)** · `obs:assayer:intent-one-corrupted-label-in-two-hundred-costs-almost-nothing-asymmetric-flip`

Symmetric corruption at one half per cent under total separation lowers observed AUC by only about one half of one hundredth, because flips in the two directions largely cancel in the pairwise count. Flipping one benign label in a twenty-per-cent-adverse block instead puts a known count of rows on a known side, which is what makes the loss follow from the class tallies and one measured placement rather than from tuned model output.

**Observation (The pulse phase decides both the allowance and the rank placement)** · `obs:assayer:intent-one-corrupted-label-in-two-hundred-costs-almost-nothing-pulse-phase`

Putting the corrupted row first leaves a full block for the Page allowance to erase it before observation; putting it last would leave a transient value near one at every checkpoint and test phase rather than accumulation. The phase decides a second thing the earlier reasoning did not weigh. Because every benign request is feature-identical, a reported-benign row's score is the model's own position at that moment, and the block layout is periodic, so the corrupted rows all sit at one phase of that trajectory and their placement in the reported-benign ordering — the `q` of the precision section — is systematic rather than averaged. Where the adverse rows fall inside the block therefore moves `q` toward either end, and with it the measured loss toward either band edge. This is the open question the implementing lane settles: the declared adverse offsets are chosen so the corrupted row's phase sits near the middle of the block's score excursion, and the witness reports the measured `q` at every checkpoint so a loss near an edge is attributed to placement rather than to the mechanism under test. If a layout cannot hold `q` inside the admissible range, the layout is what changes and the promised band is not.

**Observation (Paired worlds begin after the asynchronous ramp)** · `obs:assayer:intent-one-corrupted-label-in-two-hundred-costs-almost-nothing-paired-determinism`

The standardisation steward advances on its own queue, so identical construction does not by itself give two worlds identical coordinate snapshots. Settling both ramps through the observation barrier before clean burn-in, and crossing the label barrier on both worlds at every row boundary, makes later divergence attributable to the changed valence rather than to scheduler-dependent ramp position or to one world reading a partner's unpublished label.

**Observation (The mechanisms remain behind a behavioural boundary)** · `obs:assayer:intent-one-corrupted-label-in-two-hundred-costs-almost-nothing-mechanism-boundary`

The public report exposes the consequences of forgetting and leverage but not the age or capped weight of each corrupted update, and no probe closes that gap: the probe contract admits owned projections of published model blocks, published slot moments, and a pending entry's retained inputs, none of which carries a single update's age or effective weight (´dec:harness:probe-contract´). The witness therefore keeps the promise by bounding repeated public damage and cites the separate mathematical and record-level guarantees for the mechanism; it does not add a model-state escape hatch or claim direct observation of each bad label aging out.

**Observation (Runtime is observed, never constrained)** · `obs:assayer:intent-one-corrupted-label-in-two-hundred-costs-almost-nothing-runtime-liveness`

Two online worlds require more than twenty-four thousand assess–label flushes including burn-in. Playback progress, the shared progress watcher, and the owner-panic hook make a stall fail with evidence naming the row it stopped on, while no wall-clock duration enters acceptance and no timeout is treated as a performance budget.

The class mix, corruption direction, entity rotation, checkpoint phase, public observations, and independently routed rank oracle make the promise deterministic without strengthening it beyond its stated interval. The one question the arithmetic cannot close on its own is the corrupted rows' placement in the reported-benign ordering, which the block layout fixes and the witness measures.

## Acceptance · `sec:assayer:intent-one-corrupted-label-in-two-hundred-costs-almost-nothing-acceptance`
