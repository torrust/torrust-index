# Keeping a Restored Instance on Its Interrupted Run · `plan:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from`

Keeping (´claim:persistence:a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from´) establishes that a checkpoint plus its journal is a continuation boundary: every durable Core quantity and every host-visible reading after the boundary agrees with an otherwise identical run that crossed no process restart, while incompatible coordinates select an observable cold start and numerical retuning preserves the saved state.

## What the promise says, precisely · `sec:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-precision`

Let $P$ be a deterministic prefix ending at checkpoint sequence $h$, let $J$ be the ordered labels durably appended after $h$ and before the crash, and let $S$ be a common suffix. The primary equality is between the uninterrupted execution $P \circ J \circ S$ and `restore(checkpoint(P), J) \circ S`; replay admits exactly the entries with sequence greater than $h$, preserves journal order, deduplicates a retried assessment by its first occurrence, and applies each surviving entry once, as required by (´dec:durability:checkpoint-journal´), (´cor:durability:replay-exactness´) and (´test:crate:replay-dedup-preserves-first-occurrence´).

“Model state” comprises every operational, sister, anchor and outcome-axis posterior; their means, precision matrices, maintained covariances, calibration scalars, class-rate estimates, recomputation state, drift state, marginalisation accounting, hibernation archive, feature moments, dimension map, and cold-ramp sample and position. A partial ramp resumes at its accepted count rather than returning to the priors or jumping to service (´dec:durability:ramp-resumption´), with its phase, count, base moments and empirical sufficient statistics governed by the batch-initialisation algorithm (´alg:standardisation:batch-initialisation´).

“Published risk basis” means every stable field of the public assessment payload: `RiskBasis`, outcome predictions, per-Sentinel alarms, and embedded health, excluding only the newly allocated assessment identifier (´schema:output:assessment´). A fixed probe set names the same channels, entities, reports, Sentinel IDs, identity coordinates and outcome axes in both branches.

“Ledger views” means identical Sentinel membership, dyadic keys, traffic and eligibility counts, per-axis membership, timestamps, bad-rate EWMA, and raw and compressed valence EWMAs. “Identity graph” means identical registered dimensions, graph terminal and transition counts, competitive-cell membership, cell outcome counts, per-axis membership, and corresponding EWMAs; the registration record fixes the structure whose identity is retained (´schema:keyspace:dimension-record´).

“Companion trackers” means the checkpoint-carried trackers beside the model: the calibration buffer, Platt tracker, per-dimension convergence trackers, concordance tracker, label counters, health counters and model-health accumulators. The separately branded Companion remains host-owned and outside the Core persistence boundary (´inv:guarantee:companion-independence´).

Discrete membership, ordering, sequence marks, phases, counts, flags and identifiers compare exactly. The fields `DEFAULT_TOLERANCES.default`, `DEFAULT_TOLERANCES.ewma` and `DEFAULT_TOLERANCES.auc` are respectively `1e-9`, `1e-6` and `5e-3`; `Tolerances::precision_sync_ceiling` applies the specified `1e-6` coefficient to the model width; and fields that must survive a byte-preserving round trip use `DEFAULT_TOLERANCES.exact`, which is zero. These standings come from (´tab:assayer:harness-scenario-tolerances´). `DEFAULT_TOLERANCES.standardisation`, which is `0.14`, bounds the specified store-time versus label-time coordinate mismatch in its fifty-label regime and never widens equality between the two branches.

Structural compatibility covers the construction-time signal schema in name, shape and order, the ordered interaction templates and the fixed anchor width. Any mismatch takes the cold construction path, while changing a numerical parameter such as `MonitoringConfig::h_threshold` leaves the checkpoint admissible and preserves its learned state (´dec:durability:structural-compatibility´) (´dec:construction:two-starts´).

## What the code offers today · `sec:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-code`

The shared surface is the single `Scenario` over `World`: `World::builder` or `scenario_with_config` builds it, `WorldBuilder::persistence_dirs` supplies persistence roots, the lifecycle verbs declare Sentinels, outcome axes and identity dimensions, `World::receive_report` ingests reports, `World::derive_for_request` produces a reckoning, and `World::label` accepts data built by `LabelSpec`. These are current declarations in the finished roster, not pending skeleton work (´tab:assayer:harness-implementation-library-roster´) (´dec:harness:single-scenario´).

Time moves only through forward-only `World::advance` and `World::travel_to`. Queue completion is expressed by `World::flush_observations`, `World::flush_labels`, `World::flush_identity_maintenance`, `World::flush_ledger_gc`, `World::flush_checkpoint_scheduler` and `World::drain_health_events`, each with explicit coverage and exclusions; direct `VirtualClock` movement, sleeps and polling are not witness vocabulary (´cor:clock:harness-control´) (´cor:concurrency:harness-barriers´) (´dec:harness:no-ad-hoc-waits´).

Long prefixes and suffixes use `PlaybackRow`, `PlaybackBarrierPolicy`, `PlaybackCheckpoint`, `PlaybackProgress` and `playback`, with barriers selected in their required order (´dec:harness:declarative-playback´). `World::published_model_block`, `World::published_slot_moments` and `World::pending_entry_view` return the owned `PublishedModelBlock`, `PublishedSlotMoments` and `PendingEntryView` readings that this witness needs without retaining engine state (´dec:harness:probe-contract´).

The oracle tier consists of `OracleProvenance`, `pairwise_rank`, `regularised_schur_complement`, `decay_recurrence` and `dimension_width`; none is a restart-equivalence oracle, so the witness compares the two branches rather than repurposing an analytical oracle (´dec:harness:oracle-tier´). `TrainedStateFixture` and `TrainedStateBaseline` guard the standard score-verified convergence population, not the custom persistent prefix with a partial ramp, Ledger and identity state required here (´dec:harness:guarded-fixtures´) (´dec:harness:separate-validation´). The promise is one deterministic finite scenario rather than a seed-quantified invariant, so it declares one seed and does not call `run_seeded_sweep` (´dec:harness:seeded-sweeps´).

`World::fork_persistence` and `World::fork_persistence_after` now quiesce the source, copy its checkpoint and journal into isolated storage, rebuild the restored arm under the same injected clock, and return a `PersistenceFork`. The crate-private `DurableProjection` captures every checkpoint field, normalises unordered associations and identifies the first differing field. The clean common-suffix, elapsed-decay, storage-isolation and comparator tests establish those capabilities (´cor:durability:harness-fork´) (´test:crate:persistence-fork-common-suffix´) (´test:crate:persistence-fork-decay-once´) (´test:crate:persistence-fork-journals-are-isolated´) (´test:crate:persistence-fork-comparator-names-field´).

The checkpoint payload and recovery path already carry the model families, high-water mark, Ledger and identity payloads, structural metadata, concordance, marginalisation and hibernation state, drift, standardisation, cold-ramp state, calibration buffer, convergence trackers and model-owner health counters. Focused tests prove the whole-state production path, ramp resumption, runtime-registry reconstruction, identity reattachment, journal replay and continued learning (´test:crate:checkpoint-whole-state-production-path´) (´test:crate:checkpoint-mid-ramp-resumes-where-it-stopped´) (´test:crate:checkpoint-restores-runtime-registries-with-rebind-semantics´) (´test:crate:checkpoint-restores-identity-state-on-registration´) (´test:crate:journal-replay-via-assayer-recovery´) (´test:crate:checkpoint-round-trip-end-to-end´).

**Entry (Identity maintenance already has a completion barrier)** · `entry:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-identity-barrier`

`World::flush_identity_maintenance` drains prior identity observations and deferred cache writes, publishes graph snapshots, and crosses model-owner publication for accepted lifecycle submissions before returning. It does not cover observations offered after its drain, not-yet-due periodic decay, or a lifecycle submission refused by a saturated owner queue; the witness crosses `World::flush_observations` and `World::flush_labels` separately where those exclusions matter. The maintenance checkpoint tests establish that acknowledgement follows snapshot publication and queued-observation drainage (´test:crate:checkpoint-coordination´) (´test:crate:checkpoint-drains-queues´).

No test mints or cites the kept claim. The closest test, `persistence_fork_common_suffix`, proves complete durable equality after a clean same-clock fork and a short common suffix, but it has no unabsorbed journal tail, runtime declarations, public assessment comparison, full health comparison, numerical retuning arm or structural-refusal arm. The promise therefore remains unkept.

The remaining production gap is host-visible continuity outside the checkpoint payload. Construction still resets publication numbering, the composite convergence stage, total assessments, blend statistics, assessment-degradation counters, label-integrity counters, health-event losses and drift-reset counts; the feedback-latency tracker is also reset, but that field is subject to the authority conflict recorded below.

## The witness · `sec:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-witness`

The witness is the integration target `persistence_continuation`, whose test function is `crate::a_restored_instance_continues_the_run_it_was_cut_from`. It derives an uninterrupted control and ordinary, numerical-change and structural-change rebuild arms from one guarded crash image. Its documentation takes custody of (´claim:persistence:a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from´), and its test label names that concrete scenario.

- Setup: build one seeded persistent `World` with one scalar signal, one Sentinel report, one spatial outcome axis and one identity dimension; use typed `PlaybackRow` values and a `PlaybackBarrierPolicy` ordered as observation, identity-maintenance and label barriers to drive a deterministic prefix containing both outcome classes, established graph and competitive-cell state, non-zero Ledger cells, calibration progress, positive drift residue and a deliberately partial cold ramp. A guarded baseline records the exact runtime layout, class counts, high-water mark, ramp phase and accepted count, Ledger and identity populations, calibration rows, drift reading and health counters before the cut.
- Cut preparation: call `World::flush_observations`, `World::flush_identity_maintenance`, `World::flush_ledger_gc`, `World::flush_checkpoint_scheduler` and `World::flush_labels` in dependency order, then capture the quiescent checkpoint and empty journal into isolated storage. Use `World::published_model_block` and `World::published_slot_moments` to bind the cut's public model and partial-ramp baseline.
- Control branch: retain the uninterrupted arm, derive a fixed batch, drain its cold observations, apply its labels in declared order, and then drive the common suffix through `playback` under the same barrier policy as the restored arm.
- Crash branch: rebuild the clean image, replay the runtime declarations, compare its cut baseline with the control, derive the same semantic batch using branch-local assessment identifiers, and confirm each pending context with `World::pending_entry_view`. Drain the batch's observations, call `World::shut_down_model_owner_for_test`, submit the labels so each journal append succeeds before enqueueing reports shutdown, drop the process, and fork that closed image into ordinary, numerical-change and structural-change storage.
- Restore stimulus: rebuild the ordinary arm with the original configuration, reapply the same declaration tape with the original identifiers, cross `World::flush_labels` for replay and `World::flush_identity_maintenance` for rebound identity publication, then drive the same reports, assessments, labels and suffix order as the control through `playback`.
- Observation: immediately after replay and again after the common suffix, compare the opaque normalised durable projection, `PublishedModelBlock` values, `PublishedSlotMoments`, the results of the fixed assessment probes, `Assayer::health_summary` and `Assayer::full_health_report` at their required barriers. `assert_core_assessment_near_with_drift` receives `World::tol().default`; Ledger and identity EWMAs use `World::tol().ewma`, AUC uses `World::tol().auc`, and precision synchronisation uses `Tolerances::precision_sync_ceiling`.
- Continuation assertion: require exact discrete state, field-appropriate floating budgets, a resulting processed sequence equal to the checkpoint high-water mark plus the replayed journal tail, continuation of the partial ramp's count and phase, and equal post-suffix public risk and undisputed health fields. At the post-replay cut, byte-preserved scalar fields use `World::tol().exact`.
- Numerical arm: rebuild with only `MonitoringConfig::h_threshold` changed from a non-triggering prefix value to half the positive carried drift accumulator reported at the cut. Require the unique prefix high-water mark, Ledger and identity populations, calibration rows and ramp count to survive, then require the next eligible label to take the new threshold's reset path.
- Structural arm: rebuild with the saved scalar signal changed to a cyclic shape, require a successful cold start with zero learned counters, the initial ramp position, no recovered runtime registrations and no replayed journal labels, and require the fixed probes to differ from the learned control.
- Fails-before: omitting replay loses $J$; admitting a sequence at or below $h$ double-applies evidence; dropping any durable field changes the normalised comparison; resetting an undisputed health counter changes a public report; failing to rebind runtime identities removes reports or graphs; accepting the cyclic schema restores coefficients onto different coordinates; and treating `MonitoringConfig::h_threshold` as structural erases the numerical arm's learned markers.

The witness introduces no sleeps, polls or local deadlines. `ACK_DEADLINE` remains internal liveness evidence for the named barriers and never becomes a success threshold. One declared seed is replay evidence for this finite scenario; it is not a sweep.

## What is missing · `sec:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-missing`

**Entry (The persistence fork must admit a crash tail and configuration variants)** · `entry:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-persistence-fork`

The testing fork module has the clean two-arm `PersistenceFork`, but it cannot capture a closed image whose journal has an unabsorbed tail, clone that image into several independently owned restore roots, or rebuild those roots under caller-supplied numerical and structural configurations. Extend that module with a guarded crash-image builder that owns the copied roots, preserves the one injected clock, accepts configuration transformations for each arm, and returns the measured prefix baseline described above; the builder must use the existing queue barriers and refuse an image whose checkpoint, journal tail or declarations do not match its baseline.

**Entry (Durable comparison needs an integration-safe entry point)** · `entry:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-state-projection`

The testing durable-projection module already provides the schema-exhaustive `DurableProjection` and comparator to crate tests, but integration tests cannot invoke it. Add an opaque comparison operation on `PersistenceFork` or its multi-arm successor that captures both projections after the existing persistence barriers and returns only equality or the first field-addressed difference; it must not expose precision, mutable engine state or a retained borrow, and its field categories must select exact, default, EWMA and AUC budgets rather than one blanket epsilon while checking precision-covariance synchronisation separately through the width-scaled ceiling (´dec:harness:probe-contract´).

**Entry (Restored declarations need a replayable registration tape)** · `entry:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-runtime-rebinding`

Production reconstruction and rebind semantics are present, and `World::fork_persistence_after` accepts a callback, but the shared `World` registration verbs allocate fresh identifiers and the callback therefore has to drop to raw registrations. Add a testing-world declaration value that records the original Sentinel, outcome-axis and identity registrations, reapplies their exact identifiers and process-local identity encoder to a restored arm, crosses lifecycle and identity-publication barriers, and verifies that the runtime layout did not grow.

**Entry (Host-visible health continuity needs a declared durable boundary)** · `entry:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-health-boundary`

The persistence payload and reconstruction path need to carry the undisputed host-visible state that construction currently recreates: publication version, composite convergence stage, total assessments, blend statistics, assessment-degradation counters, label-integrity counters, health-event loss count and drift-reset count. Quiescent queue occupancies remain instantaneous comparisons rather than payload fields. Feedback-latency state is not silently added here because the promise and the health record disagree about it; that STOP item must be resolved before the final comparator can claim complete health equality.

## Risks and open questions · `sec:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-risks`

**Observation (The branded Companion remains a host-side alternative)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-companion-boundary`

The working interpretation covers Core-adjacent trackers because the Companion is structurally independent. The harness currently clones its host-owned `ChallengeEffectivenessTracker` while rebuilding a persistence fork, which can support a separately composed Companion comparison without making that state part of the Core checkpoint. If “companion trackers” means the branded Companion, acceptance must compare that cloned host state separately; extending the Core payload would contradict (´inv:guarantee:companion-independence´).

**Observation (Publication identity and cumulative health are included)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-observable-boundary`

The intent catalogue says that “health metrics all match, within tolerance,” but the health record says that the feedback-latency tracker is deliberately outside the checkpoint because its stages are differences between process-local monotonic instants (´entry:health:cross-layer-latency´). The witness may neither exclude that public metric nor persist it against the record by assumption. This item is stopped until the authorities either narrow the promise or revise the durability boundary; all other health fields remain in scope.

**Observation (Elapsed downtime belongs to the adjacent decay witness)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-decay-boundary`

Holding scenario time fixed isolates state continuation from the once-only discounting promise (´claim:persistence:restore-time-decay-is-applied-exactly-once´). `World::fork_persistence_after` and the existing elapsed-decay test already cover a non-zero interval (´test:crate:persistence-fork-decay-once´); this witness uses zero elapsed time so decay cannot hide a missing or duplicated replay update.

**Observation (A warm start needs positive evidence, not build success)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-warm-start-oracle`

Both a permitted numerical change and a rejected structural change return a successfully built instance under the two-start contract. The numerical arm therefore requires several unique learned markers to survive, while the structural arm requires those same markers to be absent; construction success alone proves neither disposition.

**Observation (Each barrier proves only its own queue boundary)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-quiescence`

The finished barrier set removes the old wait risk but does not make one acknowledgement universal. `World::flush_identity_maintenance` excludes later observations, refused lifecycle submissions and cold-ramp observations; `World::flush_checkpoint_scheduler` proves only that its request entered the model-owner queue; and `World::flush_labels` is still required to complete the checkpoint and publication. The witness keeps those barriers ordered explicitly and treats any `ACK_DEADLINE` expiry as a liveness failure, never as evidence about state.

## Acceptance · `sec:assayer:intent-a-restored-instance-continues-the-run-it-was-cut-from-acceptance`

The integration target `persistence_continuation` contains `crate::a_restored_instance_continues_the_run_it_was_cut_from`, whose documentation cites the kept claim. Its report names the deterministic prefix, checkpoint high-water mark, journal tail, suffix, probe set, numerical change, structural change, declaration tape and persistence image feeding each arm.

The guarded prefix baseline passes before the cut. The ordinary arm then matches the uninterrupted arm immediately after replay and after the common suffix in the complete durable comparison, published model and slot probes, stable assessment payload, Ledger and identity state, ramp position, sequence mark, and every undisputed compact and full-health field under the field's declared comparison rule.

The numerical arm demonstrably restores the unique learned markers and applies the changed threshold on its next eligible label. The structural arm demonstrably cold-starts, omits journal replay and recovered registrations, and differs from the learned probes. Every fails-before defect reaches a named assertion without mutating production code, and the test uses no ignored case, sleep, poll or locally authored deadline.

Acceptance remains blocked only on the feedback-latency authority conflict stated above. Once that conflict is resolved and the remaining persistence and harness entries are implemented, package formatting, linting, the focused `persistence_continuation` target, all Assayer targets and features, the featureless library, documentation tests and release-mode package tests must be green with corpus-label resolution.
