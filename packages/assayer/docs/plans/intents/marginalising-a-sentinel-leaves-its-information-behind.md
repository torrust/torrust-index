# Marginalising a Sentinel leaves its information behind · `plan:assayer:intent-marginalising-a-sentinel-leaves-its-information-behind`

Keeping (´claim:lifespan:marginalising-a-sentinel-leaves-its-information-behind´) requires a lifecycle witness that separates the algebraic promise from the behavioural one: destructive Sentinel deregistration must publish the specified regularised marginal over the surviving coordinates, and held-out discrimination from those coordinates must remain observably above an untrained survivor model.

## What the promise says, precisely · `sec:assayer:intent-marginalising-a-sentinel-leaves-its-information-behind-precise-promise`

Let the learned precision before deregistration be partitioned into retained coordinates $k$ and the departing Sentinel's coordinates $r$. Exact marginalisation gives $\mu'=\mu_k$, $\Sigma'=\Sigma_{kk}$, and $B'=B_{kk}-B_{kr}B_{rr}^{-1}B_{rk}$ (´thm:gaussian:marginalisation´); the correction is the cross-information transferred from the removed block into the survivor posterior (´def:gaussian:schur-complement´).

The operational path instead computes $B'_\delta=B_{kk}-B_{kr}(B_{rr}+\delta I)^{-1}B_{rk}$ with $\delta=\lambda_{\mathrm{prior}}\epsilon_{\mathrm{Schur}}$. The defaults are $\lambda_{\mathrm{prior}}=0.1$, $\epsilon_{\mathrm{Schur}}=10^{-4}$, and a raw-condition posture ceiling of $10^8$; the arithmetic guard refuses a regularised condition above $10^{12}$, and any refusal adopts $B_{kk}$ (´alg:gaussian:regularised-schur´) (´tab:config:risk-model´).

Sentinel deregistration removes the Sentinel slot and every interaction involving it, applies that regularised marginalisation to the operational model, sister model, and each active outcome-axis model, then compacts standardisation, routing, and the dimension map (´alg:registry:sentinel-deregistration´). Destructive deregistration archives no departing payload and is the default lifecycle operation (´dec:construction:destructive-deregistration´).

The formula subtracts a positive-semidefinite correction, so $B'_\delta\preceq B_{kk}$ and a coupled retained direction loses precision relative to its conditional pre-removal block. The claim's statement that survivor precision “rises rather than falls when it goes” therefore cannot use the immediate pre-removal $B_{kk}$ as its reference; the implementable reading is that post-removal survivor precision remains above the cold prior in a trained direction while falling from $B_{kk}$. That reference defect is a STOP item for the claim wording, not permission to reverse the specified Schur ordering, which existing algebraic coverage already isolates (´test:crate:schur-vs-bkk-ordering´).

The published probe deliberately carries covariance rather than precision (´dec:harness:probe-contract´). The matrix witness therefore reconstructs each pre-removal precision from the owned covariance in `PublishedModelBlock`, applies `regularised_schur_complement` to the transition-derived Sentinel range, independently inverts the result, and compares that expected covariance with the post-removal `PublishedModelBlock`. Let $\tau_p$ be $\delta$ plus `Tolerances::precision_sync_ceiling` at the pre-removal width $p$, the same configured offset plus width-scaled inverse-pair budget used by the finished axis lifecycle pack (´test:crate:axis-lifecycle-seeded-sweep´). Each covariance entry must satisfy $|\Sigma_{ij}^{\mathrm{published}}-\Sigma_{ij}^{\mathrm{oracle}}|\leq\tau_p\max(|\Sigma_{ij}^{\mathrm{oracle}}|,1)$, while the relative Frobenius correction $\lVert B_{kk}-B'_\delta\rVert_F/\max(\lVert B_{kk}\rVert_F,1)$ must exceed $\tau_p$; the generic $10^{-9}$ tolerance is not a bound on this two-inversion route.

With no active outcome axis, one deregistration marginalises the operational and sister models, so the completion diagnostics must report two events, no skipped correction, and a worst correction-loss fraction below one. For the fixture's non-zero correction, a fallback reports a skipped correction and loss fraction one (´req:gaussian:marginalisation-error-reported´), while marginalisation itself remains infallible by adopting $B_{kk}$ (´dec:posterior:infallible-correction´).

The behavioural witness scores the same balanced held-out population before removal, after removal, and in an untrained survivor control. Each population contains at least twenty adverse and twenty benign examples, satisfying the specification's class gate (´alg:monitoring:auc´), and `pairwise_rank` evaluates all three with an exact-tie tolerance of zero. The post-removal rank must exceed the cold rank by at least one tie-aware rank quantum, $1/(2n_+n_-)$, because one tied pair contributes one half; the monitoring bands at $0.65$ and $0.10$ describe moderate discrimination and instability, not a normative Sentinel-lifecycle guarantee, so they are reported but not promoted into acceptance (´tab:monitoring:interpretation´).

The treatment begins from the guarded score-verified `TrainedStateFixture`, whose measured baseline establishes the retained signal's class counts, held-out gap, and pairwise rank before the Sentinel-specific setup (´dec:harness:guarded-fixtures´). Registering the informative Sentinel is then an incremental addition: the correlated tape supplies at least the departing slot width in eligible labels, the order-$q+1$ budget specified for learning a new Sentinel rather than the cold model's order-$2p$ budget (´prop:resource:incremental-addition´).

## The witness · `sec:assayer:intent-marginalising-a-sentinel-leaves-its-information-behind-witness`

The integration target is `lifecycle_identity_convergence`, and the proposed Rust test path is `lifecycle_identity_convergence::marginalising_sentinel_preserves_survivor_information`, matching the completed convergence split's placement of retained-information promises (´entry:assayer:harness-convergence-split´) (´dec:harness:convergence-split´).

Setup obtains the treatment from `World::trained_state` under a declared seed and builds the cold control with the same default model configuration, score-verified signal schema, channel policy, virtual-clock origin, and settled standardisation ramp, but without treatment labels. The treatment registers one Sentinel and no outcome axes, identity dimensions, competitive cells, or interaction templates; that deliberate shape makes the only Sentinel slot the complete departing range while retaining the bias, aggregate, and score-verified signal blocks.

A test-owned correlated row implements `PlaybackRow`: adverse rows ingest `golden_report`, attach a high retained score, assess through `GOLDEN_COORD`, and submit an eligible adverse `LabelSpec`; benign rows use `minimal_report`, a low retained score, the same coordinate, and an eligible benign label. `playback` drives at least the measured departing block width under a `PlaybackBarrierPolicy` ordered as `PlaybackBarrier::FlushObservations` then `PlaybackBarrier::FlushLabels`, with a final `PlaybackCheckpoint` and `PlaybackProgress` naming any incomplete row (´dec:harness:declarative-playback´). Held-out rows reuse the two populations without submitting labels.

Before deregistration, the test records pairwise rank over the held-out treatment rows, drains earlier health events, and takes operational and sister `PublishedModelBlock` readings plus the `RuntimeLayout` and departing width. It asserts that the departing width equals the complete Sentinel-slot width, that the interaction width is zero, and that the cross-block correction computed from each reconstructed precision is larger than the matrix budget; those checks make the transfer premise non-vacuous before the lifecycle stimulus.

The sole lifecycle stimulus is `World::deregister_sentinel`. That verb settles model-owner publication before returning, and each following `World::published_model_block` crosses the same named publication barrier before loading once; `World::drain_health_events` separately crosses every health producer before reading the completion event (´dec:harness:no-ad-hoc-waits´) (´dec:harness:probe-contract´).

For both operational and sister models, the matrix assertion derives the kept and removed indices from the before-and-after `RuntimeLayout`, reconstructs the pre-removal precision from the published covariance, evaluates `regularised_schur_complement` with the configured $\delta$, and independently inverts the result. It then compares the expected and published survivor covariance under the declared matrix budget, checks retained means against the corresponding pre-removal subvector, verifies $B'_\delta\preceq B_{kk}$ with a strict coupled direction, verifies one retained trained direction remains above $\lambda_{\mathrm{prior}}$, and checks that the post-removal dimension and layout shrink by exactly the departing width.

The diagnostic assertion selects the single marginalisation-completion event and requires two model events, zero skipped corrections, complete measured/bounded/unbounded accounting, and worst correction-loss fraction below one. The behavioural assertion applies `pairwise_rank` to the identical held-out examples after removal and in the cold control, requires the post-removal value to beat cold by at least $1/(2n_+n_-)$, and reports the pre, post, and cold ranks with both class counts, the incremental label budget, matrix widths, and seed.

A prior-reset defect makes the survivor covariance and mean revert toward the cold model and erases the behavioural advantage. A rows-and-columns-only defect publishes $B_{kk}$, so the Schur oracle, strict correction, and diagnostics fail even if its retained mean preserves rank. Removing the wrong coordinates fails the layout-derived dimension, kept-mean, and full-covariance comparisons. These are result-oracle failures after the guarded setup has passed, not fixture refusals (´dec:harness:separate-validation´).

## Risks and open questions · `sec:assayer:intent-marginalising-a-sentinel-leaves-its-information-behind-risks`

**Observation (The promise's precision direction needs a reference state)** · `obs:assayer:intent-marginalising-a-sentinel-leaves-its-information-behind-precision-reference`

The specified Schur formula makes survivor marginal precision no greater than $B_{kk}$, while the intent claim says precision rises when the Sentinel goes. The witness can prove the coherent pair of statements — a strict fall from $B_{kk}$ and retention above the cold prior — but it cannot make the literal event-relative rise true. The claim should replace “rises rather than falls when it goes” with a cold-reference statement before the new test cites it.

**Observation (Graceful discrimination has no lifecycle guarantee threshold)** · `obs:assayer:intent-marginalising-a-sentinel-leaves-its-information-behind-discrimination-threshold`

The specification supplies the minimum class counts and the interpretation bands, but no Sentinel-removal AUC floor or maximum loss. The tie-aware rank quantum makes “better than cold” observable without borrowing the harness's $0.005$ agreement tolerance as an effect size; if “gracefully” is meant to require post-removal AUC of at least $0.65$, loss of at most $0.10$, or retention of a fixed share of the pre-removal advantage, that stronger bound needs to be ratified where the claim is stated.

**Observation (Regularisation and fallback qualify information retention)** · `obs:assayer:intent-marginalising-a-sentinel-leaves-its-information-behind-fallback-qualification`

The two-level guarantee permits attenuation under regularisation and complete loss of the correction under fallback (´thm:gaussian:two-level-guarantee´). The fixture therefore establishes a correction above the comparison budget, and the result requires zero skipped corrections and loss fraction below one; fallback coverage remains separate rather than weakening this informative-branch witness.

**Observation (Matrix scale and rank statistics need separate tolerances)** · `obs:assayer:intent-marginalising-a-sentinel-leaves-its-information-behind-numerical-fragility`

The covariance comparison follows the existing lifecycle pack's configured offset plus width-scaled synchronisation budget, while rank uses exact pair counting and its sample-derived quantum. Neither comparison uses the generic default tolerance, and the AUC agreement tolerance is not treated as a minimum effect (´tab:assayer:harness-scenario-tolerances´).

**Observation (Lifecycle publication must be observed, not timed)** · `obs:assayer:intent-marginalising-a-sentinel-leaves-its-information-behind-publication-ordering`

Training rows cross observation and label barriers in their declared order, model readings cross the probe's publication barrier, and completion diagnostics cross `World::drain_health_events`. The witness advances no time and authors no sleep, poll, deadline, generic flush, or compound wait (´dec:harness:no-ad-hoc-waits´).

## Acceptance · `sec:assayer:intent-marginalising-a-sentinel-leaves-its-information-behind-acceptance`

The `lifecycle_identity_convergence` target contains `lifecycle_identity_convergence::marginalising_sentinel_preserves_survivor_information`, its module index names the intent claim, and its setup reports the declared seed, guarded trained baseline, departing width, eligible incremental-label count, and held-out class counts.

The test proves the operational and sister survivor covariances agree with the regularised Schur oracle under the derived matrix budget, retained means and layout compaction select the intended coordinates, the correction is non-zero and ordered below $B_{kk}$, at least one trained survivor direction remains above the prior, and the completion event records two non-fallback marginalisations.

The same held-out population yields pre-removal, post-removal, and cold-control pairwise ranks; post-removal beats cold by at least one sample-derived rank quantum. The report identifies the unresolved stronger meaning of “gracefully” rather than presenting monitoring bands or the harness's AUC agreement tolerance as a lifecycle guarantee.

The prior-reset, bare-$B_{kk}$, and wrong-coordinate defects fail the named result assertions after the trained-state guard passes. The implementation introduces no shared-harness surface and uses only playback-owned progress and the queue-specific barriers, with no sleep, poll, ignored diagnostic, or unrelated promise coverage.
