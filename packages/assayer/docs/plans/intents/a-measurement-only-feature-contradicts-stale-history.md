# Measurement disagrees with stale history · `plan:assayer:intent-a-measurement-only-feature-contradicts-stale-history`

Keeping (´claim:risk:a-measurement-only-feature-contradicts-stale-history´) establishes that normalized live Sentinel measurements and adverse outcome memory remain distinguishable at prediction time, that sparse eligible benign labels turn the distinction into model-specific drift evidence, and that elapsed-time Ledger decay reduces the discrepancy on its specified horizon.

## What the promise says precisely · `sec:assayer:intent-a-measurement-only-feature-contradicts-stale-history-precision`

Anchor position 13 is $z_{\max}=\max_s\max_a z_{s,a}$, the maximum current raw z-score over reporting Sentinels and scoring axes. It is computed as the fourteenth value of the fixed fifteen-position projection, while position 14 is the logarithm of one plus the reporting-Sentinel count (´def:dimension:anchor-projection´).

The provenance is the first invariant: $z_{\max}$ comes from the current batch report and never from the Ledger or identity outcome memory. Changing stale outcome history while holding the report fixed cannot change it; replacing an alarming report with a normalized report changes it immediately without waiting for a label (´prop:risk:anchor-measurement-only´) (´setup:extraction:from-batch-report´).

The contrasting quantity is the deepest routed cell's outcome-memory block: adverse rate, compressed valence, raw valence, and any spatial-axis pairs are read from the Ledger rather than from the report (´tab:extraction:ledger-features´) (´def:ledger:purpose´). The witness registers no identity dimension, so anchor positions 7 through 12 are zero and cannot provide a second historical path (´def:dimension:anchor-projection´).

The training target is $r_\rho=-1$ for a benign label and $+1$ for an adverse label (´def:risk:target´). For a benign probe, model $m$ grows its negative accumulator by $\max(0,\hat\rho_m+1-\kappa_{\mathrm{drift}})$ before any threshold reset, so stale-history over-prediction is measured as $\Delta S^-_m>0$ (´alg:monitoring:drift-cusums´).

The shipped allowance is $\kappa_{\mathrm{drift}}=0.1$ and the strict automatic-reset boundary is $h=10$ (´tab:config:monitoring´) (´tab:monitoring:drift-resets´). The scenario leaves the allowance at its default and sets its test-local threshold to $100$, ten times the shipped boundary, so cumulative evidence remains readable; it separately asserts that no reset event occurred.

Ledger averages apply $\lambda_L=0.999$ at each eligible label and $\gamma_{t,L}=0.999$ per elapsed hour at access. With no labels, the time factor alone takes an adverse rate to about one half after twenty-nine days, one quarter after fifty-eight, and one eighth after eighty-seven; sparse benign probes add their own factors of $\lambda_L$, so the witness compares the observed rate with the composed recurrence rather than with those pure-starvation fractions (´def:ledger:time-decay´) (´thm:temporal:ledger-decay-bound´) (´alg:ledger:all-layers-update´).

The measurable promise is therefore a conjunction. With the normalized report refreshed unchanged and a material adverse Ledger value present, consecutive sparse benign probes produce a larger negative-CUSUM increment for the sister than for the anchor; the Ledger value follows the configured label-and-time decay recurrence; and the late-horizon sister-minus-anchor increment is smaller than the pre-decay increment. The CUSUM need not fall, because its recurrence only accumulates or resets; elapsed decay removes the continuing increment rather than erasing evidence already banked.

This interpretation also fixes the role of the blend. The anchor's residual blend weight is deployment-dependent and is not an exact floor (´rem:risk:blend-mechanisms´) (´dec:risk:anchor-floor´); the witness reads the per-model drift streams, not a minimum change in the blended probability.

## The witness · `sec:assayer:intent-a-measurement-only-feature-contradicts-stale-history-witness`

The integration target `outcome_ledger` adds the function `measurement_only_feature_contradicts_stale_history`. It uses one declared seed, one channel, one Sentinel, no identity dimensions, the default decay constants and drift allowance, and the test-local reset threshold specified above.

A witness-specific guarded fixture builds a matched report pair with identical cell intervals, sample counts, coverage, coordination values, and reporting count. The training report has a quiet region and a loud region; the normalized report preserves the geometry and non-score context but sets every z-score and Sentinel cumulative-sum reading on the affected chain to its quiet value, so report shape and coverage cannot explain the result (´dec:harness:guarded-fixtures´).

The fixture settles the cold ramp, then drives a declared finite training population through `playback`. Its row type routes eligible benign labels through the quiet region and eligible adverse labels through the loud region, and its `PlaybackBarrierPolicy` selects `PlaybackBarrier::FlushLabels` at every row boundary. Before returning, the fixture records a measured baseline and refuses unless the deepest loud-cell adverse rate exceeds `Tolerances::ewma`, `RiskBasis::anchor_weight` exceeds `Tolerances::default`, the normalized request's sister predictor exceeds the anchor predictor reconstructed from `RiskBasis::rho_eff`, `RiskBasis::p_bad_sister`, `RiskBasis::kappa_eff`, and `RiskBasis::anchor_weight` by more than `Tolerances::default`, and the planned probe tranche cannot cross a calibration-refit boundary; the reconstruction follows the specified blend and probability formulas, and the named tolerances returned by `World::tol` are declared by the harness rather than fitted to this run (´def:risk:subspace-blend´) (´def:risk:probability´) (´dec:harness:declarative-playback´) (´dec:harness:guarded-fixtures´) (´dec:harness:separate-validation´) (´tab:assayer:harness-scenario-tolerances´).

The immediate stimulus ingests the normalized report at the current scenario instant and keeps routing the formerly loud coordinate. A small fixed tranche of eligible benign probes follows, with its size declared below one hundredth of the training population. For each probe the test takes the pre-label health reading, assesses the request, submits its benign label, crosses `World::flush_labels`, and takes one post-publication `Assayer::full_health_report` reading; the difference between the two owned reports is that probe's per-model CUSUM increment.

Each immediate probe must report the Sentinel, leave its report age at zero scenario time, increase the sister's negative CUSUM, and make the sister's increment exceed the anchor's by more than `Tolerances::default`. The sister probability is recorded as diagnostic evidence, but no assertion invents an anchor probability that the public assessment surface does not expose.

The test then calls `World::advance` for three successive twenty-nine-day intervals. After each advance it re-ingests the same normalized report before assessing, because otherwise report staleness would change an anchor input and confound the Ledger-decay attribution (´def:extraction:report-staleness´) (´entry:assayer:harness-scenario-time´). At each horizon it assesses the same request, reads the deepest routed Ledger rate through the proposed owned probe before labelling, submits one eligible benign label, crosses `World::flush_labels`, and reads the resulting per-model CUSUM increments.

At all three horizons `decay_recurrence` computes the expected pre-label Ledger rate from the guarded baseline, the elapsed hours, and the number of earlier benign probes. The observed rate must agree within `Tolerances::ewma`, the refreshed report must retain one reporting Sentinel with zero scenario-time age, and the eighty-seven-day sister-minus-anchor increment must be smaller than its immediate pre-decay counterpart by more than `Tolerances::default` (´def:ledger:time-decay´) (´alg:ledger:all-layers-update´) (´dec:harness:oracle-tier´) (´tab:assayer:harness-scenario-tolerances´).

The test also asserts that each model's step counter advances by the submitted probe count and that the system reset count is unchanged. These guards exclude missing-report fallback, report-age drift, asynchronous publication, automatic reset, and calibration reset as alternate explanations.

The fails-before is diagnostic. The current blended-residual implementation fails the first sister-versus-anchor differential assertion; an anchor projection that gathers a stale-history position makes the anchor follow the sister after normalization; omitting the report refresh makes the freshness guard fail; and disabling Ledger time decay makes both the rate oracle and the late differential fail.

## Risks and open questions · `sec:assayer:intent-a-measurement-only-feature-contradicts-stale-history-risks`

**Observation (The behavioural witness composes with the projection witness)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-measurement-only-feature-contradicts-stale-history-provenance-composition`

The integration result cannot attribute an anchor prediction change to position 13 alone because positions 0 through 6 also carry current aggregate measurements. The existing projection test proves the exact $z_{\max}$ computation, while the new integration test proves that the anchor's report-derived subspace diverges from the stale Ledger path. Together they keep the promise without exposing a test-only anchor feature vector or claiming that one coefficient has a guaranteed magnitude (´test:crate:anchor-subvector-computes-its-last-two-inputs´).

**Observation (The trained precondition is guarded, not discovered by the assertion)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-measurement-only-feature-contradicts-stale-history-training-margin`

Bayesian updates, standardisation, and the leverage bound make a guessed fixed convergence count fragile, while the existing `TrainedStateFixture` establishes the wrong population for this promise. The implementation chooses a finite deterministic training population, records why it clears the declared prediction-separation and adverse-rate floors, and makes the witness-specific fixture refuse with its measured baseline when it does not. No acceptance assertion rests on an exact learned probability (´dec:harness:guarded-fixtures´) (´dec:harness:separate-validation´).

**Observation (Sparse probes also update the memory they inspect)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-measurement-only-feature-contradicts-stale-history-probe-perturbation`

Every eligible benign probe applies label-indexed Ledger decay and then moves the average toward zero before the next assessment (´alg:ledger:all-layers-update´). The `decay_recurrence` oracle includes every earlier benign update as well as elapsed-time decay, and the probe-to-training ratio keeps the intervention sparse. Treating the initial Ledger value as though only the clock touched it would overstate every horizon value.

**Observation (Per-model retention is a conformance prerequisite)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-measurement-only-feature-contradicts-stale-history-per-model-conformance`

The specification admits no interpretation in which one blended residual is every model's residual: each CUSUM is defined against that model's stored assessment-time prediction. Retaining separate risk predictions and consuming the already-retained axis predictions is therefore the prerequisite. Recasting the promise as a blended-score test would make the current code testable but would keep neither the per-model monitoring rule nor the stated anchor-versus-history disagreement (´alg:monitoring:drift-cusums´) (´inv:guarantee:drift-visibility´).

**Observation (Report freshness must be restored after every time jump)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-measurement-only-feature-contradicts-stale-history-clock-scope`

`World::advance` correctly moves both clock domains and settles the time-activated queues, but it does not manufacture a fresh Sentinel report. Reusing the old arrival stamp would increase the report-staleness feature at the same horizons used to measure Ledger decay, so the witness must re-ingest byte-identical normalized content after each advance and assert zero scenario-time report age before attributing the remaining movement to outcome memory (´def:extraction:report-staleness´) (´entry:assayer:harness-scenario-time´).

## Acceptance · `sec:assayer:intent-a-measurement-only-feature-contradicts-stale-history-acceptance`

Acceptance adds the cargo integration target `outcome_ledger` function `measurement_only_feature_contradicts_stale_history`, whose documentation cites the kept claim. The implementation report names the three landed entries, the declared seed and finite training population, the guarded setup baseline, the immediate and horizon Ledger readings, the per-model CUSUM increments, the sister probabilities, the configured allowance and reset threshold, and the unchanged reset count.

The report shows that the projection mechanism test and the new public-path scenario both pass, and that the scenario fails when every model receives the blended residual, when the anchor's computed maximum is replaced by a stale-history input, when normalized reports are not refreshed after time movement, and when Ledger time decay is disabled, whether those falsifiers are demonstrated by targeted mutation or by direct identification of the assertion each defect violates.

Acceptance requires deterministic virtual time through `World::advance`, byte-identical refreshed reports, `PlaybackBarrier::FlushLabels` for the long training stimulus, explicit `World::flush_labels` barriers for probes, one owned Ledger reading per observation, `decay_recurrence` as the independent formula route, no exact learned-coefficient oracle, no widened shared tolerance, no identity dimension, no unbounded convergence loop, and no direct mutation of Ledger or model state.
