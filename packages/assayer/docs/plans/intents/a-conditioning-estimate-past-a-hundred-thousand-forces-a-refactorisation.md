# A Witness for Conditioning-Growth Refactorisation · `plan:assayer:intent-a-conditioning-estimate-past-a-hundred-thousand-forces-a-refactorisation`

This plan states what a test keeping (´claim:bayes:a-conditioning-estimate-past-a-hundred-thousand-forces-a-refactorisation´) has to distinguish: growth of the diagonal conditioning bound past its recorded baseline brings a covariance rebuild forward before the label counter does, the crossing occurs in the $10^5$ regime without making that absolute reading its cause, and the adopted rebuild leaves the precision and covariance pair within the specified synchronisation ceiling.

## What the promise says precisely · `sec:assayer:intent-a-conditioning-estimate-past-a-hundred-thousand-forces-a-refactorisation-promise`

The promise has a relative trigger and a measured postcondition. After a visit has recorded a baseline and before its effective recomputation interval expires, the diagonal ratio grows strictly past that baseline by the configured factor while the witness operates above $10^5$; that relative crossing causes one Cholesky refactorisation, the absolute figure fixes the scenario's regime rather than its cause, and the refactorisation leaves $\lVert B\tilde{\Sigma}-I\rVert_F$ near zero.

The specification separates the two conditioning quantities: $\rho(B)=\max_j B_{jj}/\min_j B_{jj}$ is an $O(p)$ lower bound on the spectral condition number rather than an estimate of it, while $\kappa(B)=\lambda_\max(B)/\lambda_\min(B)$ is measured only where a covariance recomputation is already paying for the decomposition; the public names preserve that distinction (´def:monitoring:condition-number´), (´req:monitoring:conditioning-bound-named´).

The reference configuration sets $N_\text{recompute}$ to one thousand labels and $\kappa_\text{growth}$ to two (´tab:config:risk-model´). The trigger under test is therefore $\rho_\text{now}>\kappa_\text{growth}\rho_\text{baseline}$ while the label count since the recorded baseline remains below the effective interval, not $\rho_\text{now}>10^5$ (´alg:gaussian:condition-adaptive-recompute´), (´dec:posterior:recomputation-trigger´).

The postcondition uses the whole-matrix synchronisation error and the strict adoption threshold $10^{-6}p$, where $p$ is the target model's width (´def:monitoring:synchronisation-error´), (´def:config:synchronisation-threshold´). A successful witness observes a clean factorisation, adoption of the precision and covariance together, an after-reading no worse than the before-reading and strictly below that ceiling, and no alarm (´dec:posterior:measured-adoption´).

## What the code offers today · `sec:assayer:intent-a-conditioning-estimate-past-a-hundred-thousand-forces-a-refactorisation-current-surface`

The production path remains `Assayer::label` through the label owner: each accepted label updates every Bayesian model, calls `should_recompute`, and, when an arm fires, calls `synchronisation_visit` before applying its outcome. `CholeskyConfig::n_recompute` and `CholeskyConfig::kappa_growth_factor` configure the counter and relative-growth arms, and the conditioning-growth and changed-floor-count arms force a rebuild because a good drift measurement cannot certify a precision matrix that has moved away from the state whose spectrum supplied the baseline (´dec:posterior:recomputation-trigger´).

`PrecisionHealthDetail` now exposes `kappa`, `diagonal_ratio`, `measurements`, `last_measurement_labels`, `cholesky_recomputes`, `last_sync_error`, `last_sync_error_after`, `last_rebuild_verdict`, `last_rebuild_adopted`, `n_recompute_effective`, `dimensions_at_floor`, `alarms`, and `cascade_terminus_count` through `Assayer::full_health_report`. Those owned readings distinguish a measurement from a rebuild and carry both sides of the adoption test; they do not publish the private trigger reason or the baseline ratio as separate fields.

The finished harness supplies one `Scenario` over one `World`, configured construction through `scenario_with_config`, deterministic generation through `TestRng`, `LabelSpec`, the named `World::flush_labels`, `World::flush_observations`, and `World::flush_identity_maintenance` barriers, and forward-only `World::advance` and `World::travel_to` (´tab:assayer:harness-implementation-library-roster´). Long label streams now use a subject-owned `PlaybackRow` with `playback`, `PlaybackBarrierPolicy`, `PlaybackCheckpoint`, and `PlaybackProgress`; selecting `PlaybackBarrier::FlushLabels` guarantees each checkpoint reads the row only after model publication (´dec:harness:declarative-playback´), (´test:crate:selected-label-barrier-completes-before-checkpoint´).

The owned probe tier supplies `PublishedModelBlock`, `PublishedSlotMoments`, and `PendingEntryView`; this witness uses `PublishedModelBlock::dimension`, copied with the rest of that block from one published version, while precision remains deliberately outside the probe surface (´dec:harness:probe-contract´), (´test:crate:published-model-block-is-from-one-version´). `Tolerances::precision_sync_ceiling` derives the specified ceiling at that width (´tab:assayer:harness-scenario-tolerances´). The `OracleProvenance`, `pairwise_rank`, `regularised_schur_complement`, `decay_recurrence`, and `dimension_width` tier has no conditioning-trigger or synchronisation-product oracle, `TrainedStateFixture` guards score-training preconditions rather than competitive conditioning, and `run_seeded_sweep` is available for invariant sweeps but is not needed by this single fixed-seed transition witness (´dec:harness:oracle-tier´), (´dec:harness:guarded-fixtures´), (´dec:harness:separate-validation´), (´dec:harness:seeded-sweeps´).

No current integration or crate test mints or cites this claim. The crate recomputation suite proves that an absolute ratio alone is quiet (´test:crate:conditioning-alone-never-calls-for-a-rebuild´), that the relative arms fire only on their own quantities (´test:crate:the-relative-arms-fire-on-their-own-quantities´), that an adopted rebuild leaves the fast check quiet (´test:crate:an-adopted-rebuild-leaves-the-check-quiet´), and that an isolated highly conditioned model can adopt every rebuild (´test:crate:an-isolated-model-at-the-schedules-conditioning-adopts-every-rebuild´). The public-surface floor sweep samples conditioning and recomputation counts without asserting a trigger transition (´test:integration:the-floor-moves-the-condition-estimate-and-the-recompute-count-but-not-the-error´), while the dense factorisability instrument asserts only that the geometry reaches its width without a cascade terminus (´test:integration:a-dense-competitive-schedule-leaves-every-precision-matrix-factorisable´); their live-label-path composition remains unkept, and the standing gap register still has no entry for it (´sec:assayer:testing-plan-gaps´).

## The witness · `sec:assayer:intent-a-conditioning-estimate-past-a-hundred-thousand-forces-a-refactorisation-witness`

The witness is one default-run integration test over one identified `ModelId`. It builds a configured `Scenario` with a fixed seed, a counter interval longer than the bounded post-baseline stimulus, the dense competitive identity geometry already exercised by the public instruments, and no movement of scenario time; the setup refuses to return until it has measured a finite positive baseline ratio from a clean adopted rebuild, a stable floor count, no alarm, and enough interval and ratio headroom for the promised crossing.

The stimulus is a finite sequence of subject-owned label rows interpreted by `playback` under `PlaybackBarrierPolicy::new([PlaybackBarrier::FlushLabels])`. A `PlaybackCheckpoint` after every row records the target's owned `PrecisionHealthDetail`, the current `PublishedModelBlock::dimension`, the row index, and the configured growth factor; `PlaybackProgress` supplies the first incomplete row if a barrier or checkpoint fails, and no sleep, poll, local deadline, or direct clock access is part of the witness (´dec:harness:no-ad-hoc-waits´), (´dec:harness:declarative-playback´), (´dec:harness:probe-contract´).

From the settled series, the fixture selects the first consecutive pair after the guarded baseline for which the preceding ratio is at or below $\kappa_\text{growth}\rho_\text{baseline}$ and the following ratio is strictly above both that relative boundary and $10^5$. The pair is admissible only when the row distance from the baseline is below `n_recompute_effective`, `measurements` has not advanced, `dimensions_at_floor` is unchanged, and both ratios are finite and positive; these observations exclude the counter, intervening-visit, changed-floor-count, and degenerate arms without requiring a private trigger probe.

Across that one row, `cholesky_recomputes` increases by exactly one, `kappa` carries a finite positive reading from the new rebuild, `last_rebuild_verdict` is `Some(PublishedRebuildVerdict::Clean)`, `last_rebuild_adopted` is `Some(true)`, and neither `alarms` nor `cascade_terminus_count` increases. The numerical assertion computes the ceiling with `Tolerances::precision_sync_ceiling` at the checkpoint's model width, then requires `last_sync_error_after` to be no greater than `last_sync_error` and strictly below that ceiling (´def:config:synchronisation-threshold´), (´dec:posterior:measured-adoption´).

One further playback row is the reset control. Its settled ratio remains above $10^5$ but at or below the configured multiple of the just-recorded crossing ratio, while the floor count, alarm counts, and Cholesky recomputation count remain unchanged; this distinguishes the relative arm from the retired permanently satisfied absolute threshold and is already supported at the component level by the quiet-after-rebuild test (´test:crate:an-adopted-rebuild-leaves-the-check-quiet´).

The fails-before for a counter-only implementation is a relative-boundary crossing whose recomputation count remains unchanged until the configured interval. The fails-before for a weakened adoption path is an incremented recomputation count paired with a refused or unadopted rebuild, an alarm, an after-reading worse than the before-reading, or an after-reading at or above the width-scaled ceiling.

## Risks and open questions · `sec:assayer:intent-a-conditioning-estimate-past-a-hundred-thousand-forces-a-refactorisation-risks`

**Observation (An absolute threshold is the rejected policy)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-conditioning-estimate-past-a-hundred-thousand-forces-a-refactorisation-absolute-threshold-conflict`

Rebuilding the covariance does not change the precision-matrix property an absolute threshold tests, so once such a threshold is true it remains true and calls for another rebuild on every label (´alg:gaussian:condition-adaptive-recompute´). The settled policy and its crate witness instead compare growth with the recorded baseline (´test:crate:conditioning-alone-never-calls-for-a-rebuild´); arranging only $\rho(B)>10^5$ and observing a rebuild therefore supplies no cause.

**Observation (The competing arms can counterfeit causality)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-conditioning-estimate-past-a-hundred-thousand-forces-a-refactorisation-competing-arms`

The counter, changed-floor-count, and degenerate-diagonal arms can produce the same public recomputation increment, and the public health surface does not publish the private trigger variant. Consecutive settled checkpoints, an adopted baseline, an unchanged measurement count and floor count, a strictly unexpired interval, and finite positive ratios are jointly required to attribute the transition to conditioning growth.

**Observation (The geometry is deterministic in stimulus and variable in trajectory)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-conditioning-estimate-past-a-hundred-thousand-forces-a-refactorisation-numerical-trajectory`

A fixed seed makes the rows replayable but does not fix parallel floating-point reduction order or the label at which competitive cells publish. The guard and result assertion therefore use inequalities over a bounded playback and report the last checkpoint rather than pinning a label ordinal, true condition number, or exact after-reading; `PlaybackProgress` identifies a stalled row but establishes no latency promise (´cav:harness:progress-not-latency´).

**Observation (The witness belongs outside the ordinary fast tier until measured)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-conditioning-estimate-past-a-hundred-thousand-forces-a-refactorisation-runtime-tier`

The existing dense instruments take minutes because they grow matrices into the thousands, but the keeping witness must remain in the default package run. Its bounded playback should use the narrowest measured geometry that reaches the guarded relative crossing; if that cost is not acceptable, the fixture must be narrowed, and the implementation report records the isolated and package-test wall times.

## Acceptance · `sec:assayer:intent-a-conditioning-estimate-past-a-hundred-thousand-forces-a-refactorisation-acceptance`

The `posterior_convergence` integration target contains and indexes `posterior_convergence::conditioning_growth_past_its_baseline_forces_an_early_refactorisation`, the posterior-conditioning home selected by the convergence split (´dec:harness:convergence-split´). The function cites and keeps the claim, uses the shared `Scenario`, guarded setup, declarative playback, label barrier, owned readings, and named tolerance surface, and remains part of the default package test run.

Its failure report identifies the target model, seed, baseline row, preceding row, crossing row, reset-control row, baseline and observed ratios, growth factor, labels absorbed against `n_recompute_effective`, measurement and floor counts, recomputation counts, true condition number, verdict, adoption, alarm and cascade deltas, before-reading, after-reading, model width, synchronisation ceiling, and the first incomplete or final checkpoint when setup fails.

The keeping assertions show that the relative crossing occurs above $10^5$ before the counter can fire, causes exactly one rebuild with no competing-arm evidence, leaves the next-label reset control quiet, and installs a pair whose after-reading is no worse than its before-reading and strictly below $10^{-6}p$. The isolated test command, the package test command, their exit statuses, and their wall times complete the implementation report.
