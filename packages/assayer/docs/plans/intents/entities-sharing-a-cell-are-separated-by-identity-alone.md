# Entities sharing a cell are separated by identity alone · `plan:assayer:intent-entities-sharing-a-cell-are-separated-by-identity-alone`

Keeping (´claim:identity:entities-sharing-a-cell-are-separated-by-identity-alone´) establishes that two subjects with indistinguishable Sentinel geometry acquire a useful risk ordering from identity features while their one shared spatial outcome average continues to describe only the balanced population.

## What the promise says, precisely · `sec:assayer:intent-entities-sharing-a-cell-are-separated-by-identity-alone-promise`

The fixture contains two stable entity keys, one identity dimension, one channel, one reporting Sentinel, no declared signals, and no outcome axes. Both entities carry the same Sentinel identifier and coordinate through every assessment, while the identity encoder places their keys in opposite halves of its domain; the encoding follows the host contract (´req:keyspace:encoding-contract´), and the resulting cell indicators are the features on which the model learns per-range weights (´def:keyspace:competitive-indicators´).

The Sentinel report contains only its permanent root cell. Every label therefore updates the same Ledger entry, whose role is the adverse rate of all traffic routed through the Sentinel rather than either entity's rate (´def:ledger:root-semantics´); this is the intended separation between Sentinel spatial memory and identity state (´tab:ledger:versus-identity´).

The stimulus is exactly five hundred alternating assess-label cycles, beginning with the benign entity and ending with the adverse entity, so each entity contributes two hundred and fifty labels and the shared population is exactly balanced. Every label records `Action::Allow`, which admits both classes to the sister, anchor, and operational models alike under the eligibility table (´tab:eligibility:training´).

Discrimination is the tie-corrected pairwise rank probability over the assessment-time `p_bad` readings, with one population for each outcome class as in the specified AUC computation (´alg:monitoring:auc´). The witness requires a strict `auc > 0.65`. The standing correction fixes the shape of that requirement rather than its value: it replaces “close to one” with an ordering materially above one half beside a cell average near one half, because a threshold that unlucky sampling alone can miss carries no information when it fails (´obs:assayer:reframe-identity-threshold´). The value is the lower edge of the moderate band, and that table states of its own bands that they are reading guidance and fix nothing (´tab:monitoring:interpretation´), so the floor is this witness's threshold and not a product contract. The same floor and the same held-out gap are already carried by the five-hundred-label public convergence witness (´test:integration:scalar-signal-population-split-converges-directionally´), which takes its rank over a held-out sample where this witness takes it over the assessment-time stream; what the two share is the floor, not the estimator.

The post-training point estimate supplies a second quantity: the adverse entity's held-out `p_bad` exceeds the benign entity's by more than `0.10`. This prevents a chronology-driven prequential rank from standing in for separation in the model that exists after the five-hundredth label.

The spatial control is `abs(root_adverse_rate - 0.5) < 0.01`. The fixture sets `ledger.lambda_l` on `AssayerConfig` to `0.99`, which lies inside the configured open unit interval (´tab:config:ledger´); the specified per-entry update averages the positive-valence indicator at that rate (´tab:ledger:entry-state´), so a stream that starts from zero, alternates, and closes each pair on the adverse label has the exact five-hundred-label endpoint `(1 - 0.99^500) / (1 + 0.99)`, near `0.499211`. The specification's own closed form is written for a stream at a true rate, `p(1 - lambda_l^n)`, and at one half it gives `0.496715` over the same horizon (´data:ledger:attenuation´): the two agree to two decimals and part in the third, and it is the alternating form that a deterministic tape can be held to under an exact-arithmetic tolerance. Both satisfy the near-half control, which therefore follows from the declared rate rather than from a fitted observation.

The identity dimension uses competitive depth cutoff one, the root plus two depth-one branches, and the default identity outcome EWMA rate `0.95`; one is the least cutoff the parameter table admits and the rate is that table's default rather than a fixture choice (´tab:config:identity´). The two branch indicators and the cross-dimension maximum adverse-rate feature are identity inputs (´tab:keyspace:cross-dimension-features´), while every non-identity input remains identical between the two classes.

No standing test mints or cites this claim, so the promise remains unkept and the coverage report still lists it as an intent (´sec:assayer:intents´). The scalar-signal convergence test establishes the five-hundred-label numerical precedent without identity or shared Sentinel geometry (´test:integration:scalar-signal-population-split-converges-directionally´), while the nearest identity test establishes that a keyed dimension's association reading separates from a null dimension without asserting posterior risk separation or the balanced Ledger control (´test:integration:the-association-reading-separates-a-keyed-dimension-from-a-null-one´).

## What the code offers today · `sec:assayer:intent-entities-sharing-a-cell-are-separated-by-identity-alone-code`

The unified harness exposes `scenario_with_config`, `World`, and the public-path verbs this witness drives: `World::register_sentinel`, `World::receive_report`, `World::request_with_sentinel`, `World::assess`, `World::derive_for_request`, `World::label`, `World::settle_cold_ramp_with`, `World::publish_lifecycle`, `World::flush_observations`, `World::flush_labels`, and `World::flush_identity_maintenance`. All are current entries in the finished library roster (´tab:assayer:harness-implementation-library-roster´), and a test waits by calling the barrier named for the queue whose completion it needs and in no other way (´dec:harness:no-ad-hoc-waits´).

`World::assayer` reaches the public `Assayer::register_identity_dimension`, which takes a shared reference, so a test can declare an `IdentityDimensionRegistration` with its own `domain_bits`, `depth_cutoff`, `encode` function, and `IdentityBudget`; `IdentityBudget::for_depth_cutoff` derives the graph's creation depth, eviction depth, and split threshold from that one cutoff. `World::publish_lifecycle` then returns only once a lifecycle submission has traversed the handler and its snapshot has been published, because the model-owner checkpoint that follows shares the same first-in-first-out command queue.

`World::register_identity_with_cells` is the landed guarded route to an identity geometry: it takes a `CompetitiveCellSpec` of declared observations and an exact expected competitive set, drives the observations through assessment, crosses the identity-maintenance barrier, reads the published dimension map once, and refuses with both sets when the published layout is not the declared one (´entry:assayer:harness-complete-builder´) (´dec:harness:guarded-fixtures´). It registers through the conventional harness encoding — the first eight bytes of the entity key read as a little-endian integer and zero-extended — at a registration depth cutoff of ten, and only the budget is caller-declared. A dimension whose two keys sit in opposite halves of a 128-bit domain under a depth-one cutoff is therefore not expressible through it, which is why this witness registers directly and carries its own guard.

**Entry (Deterministic identity-maintenance barrier)** · `entry:assayer:intent-entities-sharing-a-cell-are-separated-by-identity-alone-identity-barrier`

This dependency is complete as `World::flush_identity_maintenance` (´entry:assayer:harness-closed-barriers´). The maintenance checkpoint drains identity observations and deferred signal-cache writes, detects competitive-set changes, offers their lifecycle submissions, and publishes graph snapshots before acknowledging; the model-owner publication barrier is sent only after that acknowledgement, so every submission accepted from the cycle has been applied and published when the verb returns. Its stated exclusions are observations offered after the drain, periodic identity decay not yet due, and submissions a saturated model-owner queue refused — and a geometry those exclusions leave short is reported by the fixture guard rather than chased with a further wait.

Once those barriers are crossed, `Assayer::full_health_report` returns an owned `SystemHealthReport`. Its `ledger.per_sentinel` entry carries the `SentinelLedgerHealth` fields this witness reads — `entry_count`, `max_depth`, `depth_histogram`, and `root_adverse_rate` — and its `identity_dimensions` entry carries `IdentityDimensionHealth` with `competitive_cell_depth_distribution` and `observations_dropped`. `World::pending_entry_view` returns an owned `PendingEntryView` for one in-flight assessment, whose `active_cell_counts` gives the active competitive-cell count per dimension; that probe copies retained fields from a single non-consuming lookup and crosses no barrier, because pending insertion completes on the assessing thread (´dec:harness:probe-contract´).

Long stimuli are subject-owned implementations of `PlaybackRow` driven by the `playback` runner under a `PlaybackBarrierPolicy`, with `PlaybackProgress`, `PlaybackBatchSize`, and `PlaybackCheckpoint` (´dec:harness:declarative-playback´). The runner owns the barrier cadence and a row adds no settling of its own, which is why `cycle_request`, whose round ends in its own label flush, is a loop body rather than a playback row. Progress names the first incomplete row for liveness and is not a reading of elapsed time (´cav:harness:progress-not-latency´).

`pairwise_rank` is the shared rank oracle: it takes labelled scores, a class-count gate, and a tie tolerance, awards one for a correctly ordered pair and one half for a tie within that tolerance, and returns nothing when either class falls under the gate. Its declared provenance is the specification formula, evaluated as the pairwise reading of rank discrimination rather than through the production single-sort route (´dec:harness:oracle-tier´), and its tie arithmetic is exercised against hand-counted pairs (´test:crate:pairwise-rank-oracle-counts-ties-by-pairs´). It is the only shared rank computation the harness has: the public convergence witness still folds its own comparisons inline, so this witness takes the oracle rather than a second private fold.

`World::advance` and `World::travel_to` are the only routes to scenario time, and both settle the work their movement makes due before returning (´entry:assayer:harness-scenario-time´). This witness calls neither, so every Ledger average is read at the timestamp it was last written and the specified read-time decay contributes nothing (´def:ledger:time-decay´). `run_seeded_sweep` is likewise not this witness's shape: the subject is one deterministic tape at a declared seed, not an invariant quantified over generated inputs (´dec:harness:seeded-sweeps´).

At label time the stored entity key is re-encoded, the indicator features are reconstructed from the current competitive set, and the outcome averages are written to the cells recorded at assessment time that are still competitive (´alg:runtime:identity-outcome-update´) (´alg:runtime:update-path´) (´dec:vector:label-time-assembly´). Settling the final layout before the measured tape is what keeps that reconstruction out of the class distinction under test.

`TrainedStateFixture` is not this witness's starting state: it guards a score-signal population that has already been trained, where this claim is about the identity-only training trajectory itself. The guarded-fixture rule applies all the same, through a subject-local fixture that returns only once the shared Sentinel root, the final identity geometry, the class-independent request shape, and the zero-label baseline have been measured, and that stays separate from the oracle reading the result (´dec:harness:guarded-fixtures´) (´dec:harness:separate-validation´).

## The witness · `sec:assayer:intent-entities-sharing-a-cell-are-separated-by-identity-alone-witness`

The `lifecycle_identity_convergence` integration target is the destination for `lifecycle_identity_convergence::entities_sharing_a_cell_are_separated_by_identity_alone`, with its claim and test labels added to that target's index; the target already exists and carries no witness yet (´dec:harness:convergence-split´).

Setup builds an explicit `AssayerConfig` through `scenario_with_config` with `ledger.lambda_l = 0.99`, one default channel, a fixed seed, and otherwise ordinary harness values. It registers one Sentinel through `World::register_sentinel` and ingests `minimal_report` through `World::receive_report`, so the Sentinel's Ledger holds its root entry and nothing else. It then declares one `IdentityDimensionRegistration` over 128 domain bits at depth cutoff one, with `IdentityBudget::for_depth_cutoff(1)` and an `encode` function mapping the benign and adverse keys to opposite most significant bits, submits it through `World::assayer`, and crosses `World::publish_lifecycle` before the first observation.

Twelve unlabelled requests, six for each key, are then offered through `World::assess`, and one `World::flush_identity_maintenance` drains them and publishes the layout they produce. Twelve is the smallest balanced total exceeding the budget's split threshold of ten, which is the accumulated unit-weight intensity a cell must pass before it subdivides; that the root did subdivide is settled by the guard below rather than by the count.

The subject-local `IdentitySeparationFixture` then reads one owned `SystemHealthReport` and one owned `PendingEntryView` for each of two guard assessments. It requires one Sentinel Ledger entry at depth zero with `root_adverse_rate` at zero, a competitive cell depth distribution of one root and exactly two depth-one branches, two active identity cells for each guarded request — the root and that key's own branch, which is the whole active chain at this cutoff — equal Sentinel coordinates in the two request constructors, no signals, no outcome axes, no labels, and no dropped identity observations. A failure reports the whole measured baseline, so a setup that never formed the geometry stays distinguishable from a model that was given it and failed to learn (´dec:harness:guarded-fixtures´).

`World::settle_cold_ramp_with` then retires the standardisation prior against the same two request shapes; the ramp offers its own bursts, crosses the observation barrier on each, and returns only once standardisation is in service. A following `World::flush_identity_maintenance` confirms that the ramp's assessments left the root-plus-two-branch layout unchanged. Scenario time stays fixed throughout, so the witness calls neither time verb and no elapsed-time decay enters any average.

The measured tape is five hundred `IdentityCycleRow` values alternating benign then adverse for two hundred and fifty pairs. Each row builds the same channel, Sentinel, and coordinate through `World::request_with_sentinel`, calls `World::derive_for_request`, appends the returned pre-label `assessment.risk.p_bad` and its class to the tape's own collector — a row reports no value to the runner — and submits `LabelSpec::benign` or `LabelSpec::adverse` through `World::label`. The row crosses no barrier of its own; `playback` runs it under a `PlaybackBarrierPolicy` of `PlaybackBarrier::FlushLabels` with a `PlaybackProgress` and a bounded `PlaybackBatchSize`, so each row's label is published before the next row assesses (´dec:harness:declarative-playback´).

The owned health report is taken as soon as `playback` returns and before anything further is assessed, so the root average is read over exactly the five hundred labels. `pairwise_rank` then folds the five hundred collected scores under the configured `monitoring.min_auc_class_count` with `World::tol().exact` as the tie tolerance, which credits a tie only between bit-identical readings. Two fresh unlabelled `World::derive_for_request` calls supply the post-training benign and adverse risks. The shared oracle stays separate from the fixture guard because a setup failure and a discrimination failure call for different remedies (´dec:harness:oracle-tier´) (´dec:harness:separate-validation´).

The assertions require finite in-range risks, `auc > 0.65`, an adverse-minus-benign held-out risk above `0.10`, an observed root adverse rate within `World::tol().ewma` of `(1 - 0.99^500) / (1 + 0.99)`, the unchanged one-entry root-only Ledger, the unchanged root-plus-two-branch identity geometry, no dropped observations, and clean engine health. That endpoint is itself within `0.01` of one half, so comparing against the recurrence keeps the stated spatial control and tightens it.

The fails-before is an implementation that zero-fills identity indicators and identity aggregates, omits the identity block from assembly, or maps both keys to the same active branch. Both classes then present the same risk features, the rank falls to chance and the held-out gap collapses, while the shared Sentinel root still follows the balanced recurrence; the witness therefore fails on identity separation rather than on the control.

## Risks and open questions · `sec:assayer:intent-entities-sharing-a-cell-are-separated-by-identity-alone-risks`

**Observation (The promised ceiling becomes an informative floor)** · `obs:assayer:intent-entities-sharing-a-cell-are-separated-by-identity-alone-threshold`

“Close to one” has no numeric boundary and would turn ordinary finite-sample movement into an uninformative failure, which is the correction's argument for an expectation this witness could fail on the merits (´obs:assayer:reframe-identity-threshold´). The correction fixes that shape and not the number: `0.65` is the lower edge of a moderate band whose own table fixes nothing (´tab:monitoring:interpretation´) and the floor the existing public convergence witness already carries, so it is this witness's threshold and never a product contract. The independent held-out gap is what stops the lower floor from accepting a ranking that existed only early in the stream.

**Observation (The near-half control requires a fixture rate)** · `obs:assayer:intent-entities-sharing-a-cell-are-separated-by-identity-alone-ledger-rate`

At the default `lambda_l = 0.999` the same recurrence leaves an alternating rate from zero near `0.197` after five hundred labels, so a literal near-half assertion would contradict the promise's horizon rather than test it. The configured `0.99` is inside the specified domain (´tab:config:ledger´) and puts the endpoint near `0.499211`, and comparing the observed rate against that endpoint under `World::tol().ewma` is what makes the control a derivation rather than a tolerance fitted to a run. The specification's closed form for a stream at a true rate would sit at `0.496715` over the same horizon (´data:ledger:attenuation´), which that tolerance rejects, so the assertion names the alternating form the tape actually produces.

**Observation (Geometry settlement is state-based)** · `obs:assayer:intent-entities-sharing-a-cell-are-separated-by-identity-alone-geometry-settlement`

`World::flush_identity_maintenance` replaces the old traffic-driven wait, but it cannot make a refused lifecycle submission visible and `PendingEntryView` exposes active-cell counts rather than cell identifiers. The guard therefore combines the acknowledged barrier, the exact health depth distribution, the declared encoder and cutoff, and the two pending-entry counts; any mismatch is a fixture refusal, never a reason to poll or add traffic (´dec:harness:probe-contract´). A depth-one creation depth also closes the geometry: once the two branches exist no further competitive cell can be created, so the specified divergence between the stored cells an outcome update writes to and the current cells an indicator is rebuilt from cannot open mid-tape (´cav:limitation:competitive-churn´). The price of registering outside the gated verb is that this fixture restates part of what that verb already establishes; a shared verb accepting a declared encoding and cutoff would retire the duplication.

**Observation (Warm-up cannot carry the outcome)** · `obs:assayer:intent-entities-sharing-a-cell-are-separated-by-identity-alone-warmup-purity`

The setup assessments are unlabelled and therefore advance observational geometry and standardisation but no outcome-learned state (´dec:ordering:evidence-authority´). Settling the cold ramp after the guarded geometry ensures both held-out entities are compared in one final coordinate system rather than across the finite prior-mass transition (´dec:vector:prior-mass-ramp´).

**Observation (Alternation controls chronology)** · `obs:assayer:intent-entities-sharing-a-cell-are-separated-by-identity-alone-chronology`

Grouping all benign labels before all adverse labels would let general learning progress correlate with class and inflate a rank statistic taken over the assessment-time stream. Pairwise alternation balances chronology at every adjacent pair, and because this witness ranks the stream where the public convergence precedent ranks a held-out sample, the post-training gap is the control that precedent has no need of.

## Acceptance · `sec:assayer:intent-entities-sharing-a-cell-are-separated-by-identity-alone-acceptance`

Acceptance lands the integration target `lifecycle_identity_convergence` carrying `lifecycle_identity_convergence::entities_sharing_a_cell_are_separated_by_identity_alone`, its claim and test labels, and its target-index row.

The implementation report names every changed integration-test item, the fixed seed, the identity registration with its encoder and budget, the Sentinel coordinate, the Ledger rate, the geometry-tape derivation, the label and class counts, the AUC floor, the held-out gap, the analytical root-rate endpoint and its tolerance, the geometry predicate, and the playback barrier policy.

The report shows the observed rank figure, both held-out risks and their gap, the observed and expected root adverse rates, the Sentinel Ledger entry count and depth, the identity depth histogram, the pending-entry active-cell counts, the dropped-observation count, and the final health verdict.

The report includes a red result from the identity-blind fails-before or an equivalent deliberate weakening, a green result after the witness lands, and the required formatting, lint, package integration, featureless-library, documentation, and release-test gates with their commands and verdicts.

Acceptance requires the fixed-seed playback to complete without a sleep, a poll, a local deadline, or widened production visibility; exactly five hundred measured labels with balanced classes; a single shared Sentinel root throughout; one identity root and exactly two depth-one branches before the first measured label; a rank figure above `0.65`; held-out separation above `0.10`; the observed root rate within `World::tol().ewma` of the alternating recurrence; and that endpoint within `0.01` of one half.
