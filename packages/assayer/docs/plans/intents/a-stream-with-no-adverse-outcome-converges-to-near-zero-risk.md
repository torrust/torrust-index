# A benign stream converges at the reference default over its honest length · `plan:assayer:intent-a-stream-with-no-adverse-outcome-converges-to-near-zero-risk`

The promise at (´claim:bayes:a-stream-with-no-adverse-outcome-converges-to-near-zero-risk´) holds the reference configuration fixed and gives the recurrence its honest length: sustained non-positive evidence drives both class-rate estimates into the near-zero ceiling regime without collapsing the posterior, while the next adverse operational update remains bounded by its leverage.

## What the promise says, precisely · `sec:assayer:intent-a-stream-with-no-adverse-outcome-converges-to-near-zero-risk-promise`

For an eligible segment with $v_i\leq0$, both positive-rate trackers follow $P_{+,N}=\gamma^N P_{+,0}$ because the tracker moves before the label's own weight is derived (´alg:weighting:tracker-update´). The reference configuration gives $P_{+,0}=0.5$, $\gamma_\text{opr}=0.9995$, and $\gamma_\text{inh}=0.9998$ (´def:weighting:initial-value´) (´tab:config:risk-model´). The first whole-label horizons below the specified approximate $0.005$ importance-ceiling boundary are therefore $N_\text{opr}=\lceil\log(0.005/0.5)/\log(0.9995)\rceil=9{,}209$, where $0.5(0.9995)^{9{,}209}\approx0.00499759$, and $N_\text{inh}=\lceil\log(0.005/0.5)/\log(0.9998)\rceil=23{,}024$, where $0.5(0.9998)^{23{,}024}\approx0.00499955$.

The positive importance weight is $w_+=\min(1/(2P_++\varepsilon),100)$, so the configured ceiling binds below approximately $0.005$ (´def:weighting:balancing-weights´). After $23{,}024$ benign labels the operational tracker is approximately $4.99\times10^{-6}$; its next positive update remains below the boundary and requests the ceiling, while the eligible tracker's positive update moves back above the boundary and must be checked against its recurrence rather than reported as a ceiling hit.

The near-zero quantity promised here is the pair of class-rate trackers at the importance-ceiling boundary. The risk probability is the calibrated sigmoid image of the effective raw estimate (´def:risk:probability´), and neither that definition nor the claim supplies a numerical near-zero threshold for `RiskBasis::p_bad`, `RiskBasis::p_bad_operational`, or `RiskBasis::p_bad_sister`. The witness therefore reads those fields for finite, interior probabilities and for the adverse injection's direction of movement, not against an invented probability ceiling.

Non-degeneration means every floating field in `RiskBasis` is finite, each probability remains strictly inside $(0,1)$, `RiskBasis::uncertainty` and `RiskBasis::kappa_eff` remain positive, every available `PrecisionHealthDetail::lambda_min` and `PrecisionHealthDetail::kappa` is finite and positive, `PrecisionHealthDetail::floor_share` remains below one, no precision alarm or cascade terminus appears, and `PrecisionHealthDetail::last_sync_error_residual` stays within `Tolerances::precision_sync_ceiling` at that model's width. These checks exercise the public basis (´schema:risk:basis´), the maintained spectral floor (´dec:posterior:spectral-floor´), and the specified dimension-scaled synchronisation threshold (´def:monitoring:synchronisation-error´).

For the first $v>0$ label after the segment, let $h_\text{policy}=\hat\phi^{\mathsf T}(\Sigma/f_t)\hat\phi$, $w_*=\min(w_\text{target},100)$, and $L=c/(h_\text{policy}+\varepsilon)$. The virtual clock remains fixed, so $f_t=1$ and the policy leverage equals the stored-covariance leverage. The promised protection is the strict relation $L<w_*$ and effective weight $w_\text{eff}=L$, followed by a finite positive movement in the held-out injection-direction probability; the three-way minimum is the update algorithm's (´alg:update:sherman-morrison´), and the precision-ratio protection is the leverage proposition's (´prop:update:leverage-bound´).

The ordering is load-bearing: the policy leverage and limiting weight are read before mutation, never inferred from a repaired posterior (´dec:posterior:leverage-before´). An importance-ceiling hit alone is not evidence that leverage fired because the two caps protect different hazards (´disc:weighting:leverage-interaction´). The standing testing-plan debt remains the deeper convergence gap (´entry:assayer:gap-convergence-depth´).

## The witness · `sec:assayer:intent-a-stream-with-no-adverse-outcome-converges-to-near-zero-risk-witness`

This distinguishes a crossing from merely observing a later value below the boundary, and the independent route carries the oracle tier's provenance (´dec:harness:oracle-tier´).

The assessment identifier prevents another update from satisfying the assertion.

- Fails-before: a wrong decay or tracker-before-weight ordering misses a scaled checkpoint or a first-crossing predecessor; an uncapped reciprocal weight disagrees with the requested ceiling; omitting the leverage term makes the injected effective weight equal one hundred instead of $L$; and a broken posterior update produces a non-finite basis or model block, an unhealthy spectrum, excess synchronisation residual, a cascade, or a stopped label path.

## Risks and open questions · `sec:assayer:intent-a-stream-with-no-adverse-outcome-converges-to-near-zero-risk-risks`

**Observation (The reference-default horizon is fixed by the recurrence)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-stream-with-no-adverse-outcome-converges-to-near-zero-risk-default-horizon`

Starting at $0.5$, the reference rates yield approximately $0.0410$ globally and $0.1839$ on the eligible tracker after five thousand benign labels, both above the $0.005$ boundary. The witness therefore follows the specified recurrence to the first whole-label crossings, $9{,}209$ globally and $23{,}024$ on eligible traffic; no configured-rate, pre-seeded-rate, or trained-fixture shortcut substitutes for that length.

**Observation (An adverse-free buffer cannot calibrate itself)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-stream-with-no-adverse-outcome-converges-to-near-zero-risk-calibration-floor`

A calibration regime refits only after at least thirty weighted records and three outcomes of each class (´req:platt:minimum-samples´). An adverse-free segment beginning from the reference tracker state cannot satisfy that class precondition, so the witness does not claim that its probability has been newly calibrated to a numerical near-zero threshold. It checks probabilities only for numerical health and injection direction; adding a two-class prefix would change the tracker initial condition and invalidate the honest crossing calculation.

**Observation (The next adverse label needs a declared direction)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-stream-with-no-adverse-outcome-converges-to-near-zero-risk-injection-geometry`

Repeated benign labels concentrate covariance along their own feature direction, so another label on exactly that direction can be ceiling-limited without being leverage-limited. Holding the scalar score fixed and toggling the dormant verification bit keeps the request population legible while supplying a direction the benign segment did not train. If the trace reports $L\geq100$, the declared geometry did not establish the promise and the fixture must fail rather than substitute a fitted request.

**Observation (The reference tier needs measured execution routing)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-stream-with-no-adverse-outcome-converges-to-near-zero-risk-runtime`

Playback batching bounds materialisation but deliberately preserves both queue barriers at every row, so the $23{,}024$-label witness may be expensive (´test:unit:batch-size-preserves-mid-batch-publication-boundaries´). The implementation records scaled and reference runtimes before deciding execution routing. Until a maintained non-skipping long-run command is established, the full function remains a normal named test as well as directly selectable on demand; no environment guard, authored sleep, local poll, or skipped-test attribute may turn the honest horizon into an unexecuted branch.

**Observation (Near-zero and settled need margins, not endpoint equality)** · `obs:assayer:intent-a-stream-with-no-adverse-outcome-converges-to-near-zero-risk-numerical-margin`

The tracker assertion uses the named EWMA budget around independently computed values and proves the boundary with adjacent whole-label horizons, rather than requiring floating endpoint equality. The sigmoid never reaches zero at a finite raw estimate and the spectral floor deliberately prevents posterior collapse, so zero probability, zero uncertainty, a fitted settling band, and monotonic probability at every label are not acceptance conditions.
